I am "414", from New York. I enjoy honest, good-faith, clear discussion of political and ethical philosophy.

The goal of this site is to provide an exhaustive repository of my values, their justifications, and their defenses - with utmost consistency.

Though I wish it were true, the world I wish for would never be reality. On this site, I lay out my values and thoughts on what policy toward that world might look like. The path we are currently treading as a nation does not inch closer to the nation I wish for, so I favor any directional moves that close the distance to the ideal, though they are not the ideal itself.

Voting for and supporting parties and policies that are directionally aligned is the realistic goal that I follow as a cost analysis. They are very rarely perfect and will likely never match my complete worldview.

Rationale, definitions, rebuttals, and argumentation exist in the site's sections, with the Worldview section providing a structured overview. Some may find value in the unique blend of ideas I put forth. Begin with the Worldview section, or browse through any topic.
