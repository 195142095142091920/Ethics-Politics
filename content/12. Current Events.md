## Core Principles
I grade the current presidential administration's major actions against my positions here. Donald J. Trump took office for his second term on January 20, 2025. Each action is graded on direction as well as detail, because no administration will match my positions exactly: **Aligned** (direction and details match), **Directionally aligned (details differ)** (moves the way I do, by a different route or to a different degree), **Mixed** (parts point both ways, or I have no settled position), **Directionally opposed** (moves against my positions, with some overlap), and **Opposed**. Grades reflect my positions, set out in the sections linked from each entry; descriptions are drawn from the official text of each action.

### Immigration and Borders

>[!tldr] In short
> 6 actions graded: 1 Aligned, 3 Directionally aligned (details differ), 1 Mixed, 1 Directionally opposed.

#### Interior enforcement and removal priorities
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14159 directs stepped-up interior enforcement, detention, and removal of people unlawfully present.

I would remove illegal immigrants forcefully ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). I also require a court finding and due process in each case, which the order leaves to existing procedure.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14159: Protecting the American People Against Invasion](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/29/2025-02006/protecting-the-american-people-against-invasion)

#### Birthright citizenship order
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14160 directs agencies not to recognize citizenship for children born to parents who are unlawfully present or in temporary status.

I would also end birthright citizenship, but by constitutional amendment and going forward only, without affecting anyone who gained citizenship under prior law ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). The direction matches; the vehicle and the retroactive reach differ.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14160: Protecting the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/29/2025-02007/protecting-the-meaning-and-value-of-american-citizenship)

#### Limits on universal injunctions
**June 27, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

In *Trump v. CASA*, arising from challenges to Executive Order 14160, the Supreme Court held that federal courts generally may not issue universal injunctions.

The ruling is procedural. I keep court review of executive action ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]), and the ruling narrows the remedy without removing that review.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Supreme Court of the United States – [Trump v. CASA, Inc. (2025)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/24a884_8n59.pdf)

#### Refugee admissions suspended
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14163 suspends the U.S. Refugee Admissions Program pending review.

I treat admission as a privilege granted in the national interest, so a pause matches my direction ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). It differs in detail: I would still bar returning anyone to persecution or torture and use safe third countries ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship|Removal of Non-Citizen Residents]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14163: Realigning the United States Refugee Admissions Program](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/30/2025-02011/realigning-the-united-states-refugee-admissions-program)

#### Gold Card visa
**September 19, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally opposed**

I would admit people by compatibility with Western values and individual vetting, not by wealth, and never sell status ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Investor Visas|Investor Visas]]). Vetting still applies under the order, which is why the grade is directional rather than full opposition.

>[!quote]- Sources

#### Ending birth tourism
**August 6, 2026** · **Grade: Aligned**

Executive Order 14419 restricts nonimmigrant entry for the purpose of giving birth in the United States to obtain citizenship for the child.

This matches my goal of tying citizenship to lawful membership rather than place of birth ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14419: Ending Birth Tourism](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/11/2026-16404/ending-birth-tourism)

#### Refugee ceiling for fiscal year 2027
**September 25, 2026** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Presidential Determination No. 2026-24 caps refugee admissions for fiscal year 2027 at 17,500, allocated primarily to Afrikaners from South Africa under Executive Order 14204, and keeps the suspension and vetting rules of Executive Orders 14161 and 14163 in force.

A low ceiling matches my view of admission as a privilege granted in the national interest ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). It differs in detail: I would select immigrants by individual fit with Western values and assimilation, not by national or ethnic group ([[2. Political and Social Character#Western Values and Compatibility|Western Values and Compatibility]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Presidential Determination on Refugee Admissions for Fiscal Year 2027](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20318/presidential-determination-on-refugee-admissions-for-fiscal-year-2027)

#### Third-country removals allowed to resume
**September 29, 2026** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

In *Department of Homeland Security v. D.V.D.*, the Supreme Court stayed a district court judgment that had vacated the administration's guidance on removing people with final orders to countries not named in those orders, and agreed to hear the case, with argument set for December 9, 2026. Under the guidance, people can be sent to countries that have promised not to persecute or torture them without further steps; for other countries, the government gives notice and generally waits at least 24 hours for a fear claim. Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson would have denied the stay.

Removal to a safe third country matches my position ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship|Removal of Non-Citizen Residents]]). It differs in detail: I would never return anyone to persecution or torture, and I require notice and a real chance to be heard ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]), and a 24-hour window, or none where the country has given assurances, may fall short of that. The grade may change after the Court rules.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Supreme Court of the United States – [Order List, September 29, 2026](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/092926zr2_1a7d.pdf)
> - Supreme Court of the United States – [Docket No. 26-426: Department of Homeland Security v. D.V.D.](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/26-426.html)

#### Death penalty restored as federal policy
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14164 directs the Attorney General to seek the death penalty in appropriate federal cases and to support states that carry it out.

I go further: death is mandatory for murder and other capital crimes proven by clear-cut objective evidence ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]). Federal prosecutors keep discretion under the order.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14164: Restoring the Death Penalty and Protecting Public Safety](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/30/2025-02012/restoring-the-death-penalty-and-protecting-public-safety)

#### DC crime emergency
**August 11, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14333 declares a crime emergency in the District of Columbia and directs federal use of the Metropolitan Police Department.

Heavier policing matches my position ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Core Principles|Criminal Justice]]). I require every emergency to expire automatically on a fixed date unless renewed by the legislature ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14333: Declaring a Crime Emergency in the District of Columbia](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/14/2025-15550/declaring-a-crime-emergency-in-the-district-of-columbia)

#### Ending cashless bail
**August 25, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

Executive Order 14342 directs federal steps against jurisdictions that release defendants without cash bail.

I share the aim of detaining dangerous defendants, but I set detention by an individual finding of danger or flight risk, never by ability to pay ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Pretrial Detention and Bail|Pretrial Detention and Bail]]). Cash bail is the mechanism I reject.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14342: Taking Steps To End Cashless Bail To Protect Americans](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/28/2025-16618/taking-steps-to-end-cashless-bail-to-protect-americans)

I protect speech except true threats and incitement ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Free Speech and Censorship|Free Speech and Censorship]]). Burning one's own flag harms no one's rights; damage to others' property is already a crime without the order.

>[!quote]- Sources

### Economy, Taxes, and Trade

>[!tldr] In short

#### Reciprocal tariffs
**April 2, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally opposed**

Executive Order 14257 imposed a baseline tariff on most imports, with higher country rates, under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA).

I hold tariffs to be good in theory but questionable in practice, used cautiously and targeted at leverage, strategic industries, and national-security threats ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Trade and Economics|Trade and Economics]]). Broad baseline tariffs go beyond that. On February 20, 2026, in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump*, the Supreme Court held 6–3 that IEEPA does not authorize the President to impose tariffs, and Executive Order 14389 ended the IEEPA duties the same day. That outcome fits my position, which places taxing power with the legislature.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Supreme Court of the United States – [Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump (2026)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/24-1287%5F4gcj.pdf)
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14389: Ending Certain Tariff Actions](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/02/25/2026-03832/ending-certain-tariff-actions)
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14257: Regulating Imports With a Reciprocal Tariff](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/07/2025-06063/regulating-imports-with-a-reciprocal-tariff-to-rectify-trade-practices-that-contribute-to-large-and)

#### One Big Beautiful Bill Act
**July 4, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

Public Law 119-21 is a reconciliation law changing tax and spending; the Congressional Budget Office estimates it lowers revenues by \$4.5 trillion and direct spending by \$1.1 trillion over 2025–2034, raising deficits by \$3.4 trillion.

Lower taxes and lower spending match my direction ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Taxation]]), but I target a balanced budget and reducing the debt ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Fiscal Policy|Fiscal Policy]]), and the law adds to deficits.

>[!quote]- Sources

Executive Order 14403 establishes TrumpIRA.gov to expand access to retirement-savings accounts for workers.

I would move retirement toward private accounts with default enrollment and opt-out ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Social Security and Medicare Transition|Social Security and Medicare Transition]]). The order adds access but leaves Social Security mandatory.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14403: Promoting Retirement-Savings Access for American Workers by Establishing TrumpIRA.gov](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/05/2026-08908/promoting-retirement-savings-access-for-american-workers-by-establishing-trumpiragov)

#### Diesel fuel tax deferral
**October 5, 2026** · **Grade: Mixed**

Executive Order 14435 directs the Treasury to defer federal excise taxes on diesel fuel incurred from October 5 through December 31, 2026, without penalties or interest, using its disaster-relief authority; tells the IRS not to penalize dyed diesel used on highways in that period; and asks the Treasury to explore legislation forgiving the deferred amounts. The order cites restricted global diesel supply and high prices for farmers and truckers.

Taxing less, and an end date written into the order, fit my low-tax and time-limit rules ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Taxation]]; [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]). It conflicts in that relief goes to chosen industries rather than across the board, forgiveness would add to deficits I require to be closed ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Fiscal Policy|Fiscal Policy]]), and part of the relief supports animal agriculture, which I would end ([[9. Energy & Environment#Agriculture, Grazing, and Habitat|Agriculture, Grazing, and Habitat]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14435: Emergency Tax Relief on Diesel Fuel](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/09/2026-20855/emergency-tax-relief-on-diesel-fuel)

#### Control of Venezuelan oil exports and revenue
**January 9, 2026** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

After the capture, the United States took control of Venezuela's oil exports. Executive Order 14373 holds the sale proceeds in U.S. Treasury custody as Venezuelan sovereign property, with disbursements authorized by the Secretary of State, and shields them from attachment by creditors under a declared national emergency. The administration reported more than \$13 billion in sales by July 2026, with about \$3 billion disbursed by April for Venezuelan government salaries, oil-industry supplies, and other approved uses.

Securing the region and cutting off hostile actors matches my direction ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Interventionism vs Non-Intervention|Interventionism vs Non-Intervention]]). The details need to change to fit my position: control should defeat and contain the threat without governing the territory, and end on a fixed date unless Congress renews it ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]); creditors' claims should be settled through the courts rather than blocked by emergency ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]); the revenue belongs to Venezuelans, whose property rights hold whatever their government did, so it needs full public accounting, published audits, and spending only for their benefit; and costs to American taxpayers should be disclosed.

>[!quote]- Sources
