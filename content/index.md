This site is a researched collection of my ethics, philosophies, and normative beliefs - an honest analysis of politics and ethics leading to my conclusions.

This is my ideal for America: a negative-rights constitutional republic with a qualified franchise - epistocratic republic, not a full democracy - where only net taxpayers who pass a voter license test vote, by ranked-choice voting, with a hard criminal code, assimilation and values filters, and a thin state on tax and markets. It is a destination I am stating, not a poll of what is popular.

I am not so naive to think that ideal will ever actually be reached. It will never happen. These are ideals. Voting for and supporting things in that realm - and getting closer - is enough. Directionally aligned enough to move toward it is good. That is life.

The path we are currently treading as a nation does not inch closer to the nation I wish for. So I favor harder, more directionally aligned moves now because they close distance to the ideal. They are not the ideal itself.

Supporting parties or policies is a cost analysis. They are never perfect and will never be fully inside my worldview framework. When a side is directionally aligned on major points - such as stringently anti-crime, anti-mass immigration, pro-assimilation, futurist, non-welfarist, and fights for traditional social values - I will support them.
