On this site, I set out my ethics, philosophies, and normative beliefs as a researched collection - an honest analysis of politics and ethics leading to my conclusions on all manner of relevant topics.

Though I wish it were true, the world I wish for would never actually be reality. On this site, I lay out my ideals and decisions on what policy toward that world might look like. The path we are currently treading as a nation does not inch closer to the nation I wish for, so I favor any directional moves that close the distance to the ideal, though they are not the ideal itself.

Voting for and supporting parties and policies that are directionally aligned is the realistic goal that I follow as a cost analysis. They are very rarely perfect and will likely never match my complete worldview. 

This page is the top-level look at my worldview with descriptions describing my values. Rationale, definitions, rebuttals, and argumentation exist in the site's sections. 

---

**In one sentence:** I am a **sentientist threshold deontologist whose rights theory runs on forfeiture**. I want a **qualified-franchise epistocratic republic**, which is a modern Aristotelian polity. That republic would have:

- a **minimal, opt-in fiscal state**;
- a **maximal, forfeiture-based penal code**;
- **values-based civilizational nationalism with secular pro-American/Western assimilationism**;
- **hawkish America-First realism**;
- a **transhumanist and bioengineering program to abolish animal agriculture and reorder nature against predation**.

I vote **Republican as the best directional fit**.

**In a few words:** *Sentientist epistocratic national-individualist*. 

**In full:** *Sentientist, agnostic-atheist, negative-rightist, forfeiture-retributivist, anti-collectivist individualist, epistocratic-republican, civilizational-nationalist, classical-liberal free-market capitalist, transhumanist, futurist, predation-abolitionist anti-naturalist*.

## [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework]]

Moral anti-realist: constructivist-prescriptivist (Hume plus Hare), not relativist, and I still draw hard, enforceable verdicts

Threshold sentientist deontologist: rights are side-constraints, "not goals to maximize" ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]])

Forfeiture, not catastrophe: the threshold is the agent's own violation, not bad consequences (Moore)

Sentientist: status is substrate-neutral and graded; rights-based, unlike Singer, Regan, and Francione, with a duty to stop predation

Evidentialist scientific naturalist; a secular Stoic in personal ethics

- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Meta-Ethics|Meta-Ethics]]:** I claim no objective moral truth.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Metaphysics|Metaphysics]]:** I'm an agnostic atheist: I hold no belief in gods, but I stay open in principle to evidence.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Normative Ethics|Normative Ethics]]:** My ethics is a threshold sentientist deontology.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Moral Psychology|Moral Psychology]]:** I hold that full moral agency needs sentience plus intentional action and foresight, and that it scales with sophistication.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Animal Rights and Sentientism|Animal Rights and Sentientism]]:** Animal agriculture and aquaculture should be banned outright and shut down immediately, with sanctuary for every animal still alive ([[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Sanctuary Plan]]).
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Rescue Purchases|Rescue Purchases]]:** I believe a sentient being's right not to be killed does not disappear because a price is attached to them, so if paying is the only way to save an animal already headed to slaughter, one should pay.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Character Ethics|Character Ethics]]:** I take Stoic eudaimonism as my personal operating system for character: focus on what is within one's control, practice wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance, and keep composure while acting decisively.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Limits of Politics|Limits of Politics]]:** I believe the state exists to protect negative rights and the security of the community that sustains them, not to equalize outcomes.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Corporations as Moral Entities|Corporations as Moral Entities]]:** I see corporations as tools, not moral agents.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Duties to Future Generations|Duties to Future Generations]]:** Present agents ought not to impose extreme, unjustified risk of death or rights destruction on future sentient beings.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Aesthetics|Aesthetics]]:** I find beauty in clarity, competence, and order.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Meaning|Meaning]]:** I believe purpose is realized through exercising agency with virtue, within my framework's ethical constraints.

Civilizational nationalist: values-based and civic, not ethnic, with secular pro-American/Western assimilationism

Unlike ethnonationalists, my test is open to any ancestry

Unlike national conservatives, I am secular and define culture by values, not heritage

Anti-totalitarian parity: the state should condemn all four excluded ideologies

- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Order and Security|Order and Security]]:** Crime should be met hard: a mandatory death penalty for murder (including attempted murder), violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, large-scale distribution of hard drugs, and giving or selling any hard drug to a minor.

## [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties]]

Epistocratic constitutional republic: a modern Aristotelian polity or timocracy, close to Brennan, with a fiscal stake test and a voter license

Brennan pairs epistocracy with cosmopolitan liberalism; I pair it with civilizational nationalism and keep Swiss-style initiatives

Militant democracy (Loewenstein) without party or speech bans: bars on sworn authority roles and buildings only

Negative-rights side-constraints with forfeiture: Nozickian, plus a minimal floor that is "not a right anyone can claim"

Procedural civil libertarian, sunset-clause constitutionalist, conventional federalist

- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Individual Liberty vs State Power|Individual Liberty vs State Power]]:** The government should leave people alone unless they are actively harming others or about to do so.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]:** Emergency powers should be necessary, narrow, and temporary.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Government Structure and Checks|Government Structure and Checks]]:** Government exists to protect rights and ought to justify every expansion.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Term Limits|Term Limits]]:** I oppose term limits on any elected office.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Federalism|Federalism]]:** Defense, borders, and immigration should be federal, and schools should be local, with each state funding about \$10,000 per child that follows the child to any public school in the state.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Property Rights|Property Rights]]:** Once ownership is properly established, it should last.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Self-Defense and Defense of Others|Self-Defense and Defense of Others]]:** People should be free to use force to protect themselves or others anywhere, with no duty to retreat.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Freedom of Association|Freedom of Association]]:** People, clubs, and religious groups should be free to choose whom they associate with, but only so long as they violate no one's rights: joining or organizing a group to commit or plan rights violations, such as a conspiracy, gang, or organized violent group, ought not to be protected, and no one should be held in a group against their will.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Privacy Rights|Privacy Rights]]:** To me, privacy means control over access to one’s body, home, private spaces, and personal information.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Surveillance State|Surveillance State]]:** Facial recognition and social credit should be opt-in only.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Surveillance and Digital Technology|Surveillance and Digital Technology]]:** Data privacy should be respected and all data trading should be opt-in.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]:** Everyone should answer for what they do, and people with more power should answer to a higher standard.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Pretrial Detention and Bail|Pretrial Detention and Bail]]:** Defendants should be released before trial unless a judge finds they are dangerous or likely to flee.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Civil Asset Forfeiture|Civil Asset Forfeiture]]:** The state should not take property from people who have not been convicted, except assets of foreign cartels formally designated as war enemies or Foreign Terrorist Organizations, which would be seized as enemy property on the combatant track, and true contraband, which may be seized and destroyed or returned to owners.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Role of Juries|Role of Juries]]:** Conviction should require a unanimous jury of at least twelve, in civil and criminal cases alike.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise|Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise]]:** I support a constitutional republic with a qualified franchise, an epistocratic republic rather than a full democracy: only net taxpayers who hold a voter license, earned by passing a test on issues, civics, and laws, should vote, and ranked-choice voting and the Constitution would stay.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Voting and Elections|Voting and Elections]]:** Voters should show ID, mail-in ballots ought to be fully authenticated and encrypted, [[Glossary Index#^g-felony-and-misdemeanor|felons]] should not vote, and only net taxpayers should vote - a conflict of interest, because the franchise decides other people's money.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Election Administration|Election Administration]]:** Elections should require ID, authenticated mail ballots, and clean voter rolls, with money limited to individual donations.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Federal Enforcement Near Elections|Federal Enforcement Near Elections]]:** In my view federal enforcement around an election must follow rules fixed before it.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Redistricting|Redistricting]]:** I believe districts should be redrawn once after each census, by fixed neutral criteria, without partisan data, and never mid-decade unless a court orders it.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Ranked-Choice Voting|Ranked-Choice Voting]]:** Ranked-choice voting should be required for every federal, state, and local election, by constitutional amendment.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Primary Elections|Primary Elections]]:** I see parties as private associations that choose their nominees by their own rules.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Ballot Access and Deceptive Candidacies|Ballot Access and Deceptive Candidacies]]:** I believe a candidacy filed to deceive voters, such as a stand-in with the same name, can be kept off the ballot under a rule written before the election and decided by a court before ballots are printed.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Qualified Citizen Initiatives|Qualified Citizen Initiatives]]:** States and localities should be free to offer citizen initiatives and referendums.

Flat-tax, free-market classical liberalism with an opt-in welfare state and a Friedmanite, NIT-style floor

Sowellian on prices and disparities: markets over price controls, and outcomes explained by culture and choices, not by discrimination alone

Market pronatalist; bounded executive discretion over federal property

Unlike conservatives: no vouchers, broad tariffs, entitlement protection, or farm subsidies

Unlike libertarians: a public K-12 guarantee, a voluntary public health plan, natalist credits, and an AI-displacement trigger on capital taxes

Tariffs treated cautiously; tax and regulatory relief over mandates

- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Economic System|Economic System]]:** Markets work better than the alternatives for making people richer over time.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Socialism|Socialism]]:** I see socialism in its core sense, social or state ownership of the means of production and planning in place of markets, as antithetical to my framework and a terrible idea, because it seizes property, forces redistribution, compels labor and speech, and suppresses dissent.

- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Monetary Policy|Monetary Policy]]:** Balanced budgets keep money stable.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public-Sector Size and Civil Service|Public-Sector Size and Civil Service]]:** Government should be sized to its essential functions, with defense first.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public Funding of NGOs|Public Funding of NGOs]]:** The government ought to defund NGOs that run on public money: no grants, payment only under contracts with measurable deliverables, and no public money for advocacy.

- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Welfare and Entitlements|Welfare and Entitlements]]:** People already on benefits who followed the rules should keep their entitlement through a phase-out period.

- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Births and Family Formation|Births and Family Formation]]:** America should get back above 2.1 children per woman through support that grows with family size: a child credit that doubles from the third child, a birth credit for third and later children, and income-tax exemptions for mothers of three (half) and four (full) ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#^family-credits|family credits]]).

- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare|Healthcare]]:** People should choose between a publicly supported plan and private insurance.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval|Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval]]:** The public health option should be voluntary, with taxpayers funding it only by choice.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Private Education Oversight|Private Education Oversight]]:** Private and religious schools have the full right to exist and should be privately funded.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#K-12 Funding|K-12 Funding]]:** Each state should fund about \$10,000 per child per year for basic K-12 education, with a disability add-on set from actual need, following the child to any public school, district or charter, statewide.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Governance and Curriculum|Governance and Curriculum]]:** I would abolish the federal Department of Education and put control local first.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Homeschooling|Homeschooling]]:** Homeschooling should be fully allowed, with no school-performance testing.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Higher Education and Student Loans|Higher Education and Student Loans]]:** I would make Pell Grants opt-in, end state subsidies, and stop new federal student lending.

## [[6. Criminal Justice & Security]]

Hard retributivist through rights forfeiture, or penal maximalist, after Wellman

Punitive civil libertarian: maximal penalties under strict procedure, close to Singapore and Bukele ([[11. Modern Political Parties|S11]], [[13. News Commentary|S13]])

Unlike tough-on-crime conservatives: no qualified immunity, pretrial release by default, unanimous 12-person juries

An "objective evidence" capital standard, and a capital scope wider than any U.S. tradition

- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Evidentiary Standard|Evidentiary Standard]]:** Nobody should be executed unless the proof is overwhelming and backed by objective evidence such as video and physical evidence, not just testimony.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]:** Deliberate murder should be punished by death, and so should violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, large-scale distribution of hard drugs, and giving or selling any hard drug to a minor proven to the same strict standard.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Insanity Defense|Insanity Defense]]:** Mental illness does not undo a rights violation: a mentally handicapped person who kills still commits murder.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Lesser Homicide|Lesser Homicide]]:** A self-defense killing that went too far but was close to justified should be a lesser homicide with a lighter sentence set by the judge.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Human Trafficking|Human Trafficking]]:** I see human trafficking of adults or children, and kidnapping, as egregious rights violations that ought to be capital.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Age of Consent|Age of Consent]]:** The age of consent should be 18, except under the close-in-age exception below, and a minor cannot consent to sex with an adult.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Incest|Incest]]:** All sexual contact between blood relatives should be banned, even between consenting adults, as a misdemeanor punished by an income-scaled fine plus labor (one mark on a third offense) and a precaution against reproduction, since any child conceived would have to be carried to term.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life|Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life]]:** Repeat offenders who keep committing crimes after being punished should lose their right to life once they reach three marks, provided they are still active.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Income-Scaled Fines|Income-Scaled Fines]]:** All fines, criminal, civil, and regulatory, should be scaled to income and wealth, always paired with labor for individuals, with corporations paying up to 4% of global revenue, or their gain from the offense if higher, and the CEO personally responsible as the one ultimately responsible for the company's actions, so no designated fault-officer can take the fall.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor|Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor]]:** No one should be imprisoned for life with nothing to do.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row|Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row]]:** Capital trials should take 30 days at most, and the sentence should be carried out the day it is handed down.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Public Executions|Public Executions]]:** Public executions should return, carried out in public or openly recorded so citizens can see what the state does in their name, with hanging and gunshot both acceptable methods.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment|Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment]]:** Studies of today's slow, uncertain system cannot show whether a swift, certain death penalty deters, and the case for it rests on desert, incapacitation and forfeiture.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Wrongful Convictions and Compensation|Wrongful Convictions and Compensation]]:** Convicting an innocent person should be treated as the state violating their rights.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Juvenile Offenders|Juvenile Offenders]]:** There should be no fixed age of criminal responsibility.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Civil Commitment|Civil Commitment]]:** No one should be confined for being homeless or mentally ill.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Statutes of Limitations|Statutes of Limitations]]:** Murder, violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, large-scale distribution of hard drugs, and giving or selling any hard drug to a minor should be prosecutable no matter how much time has passed, as long as the strict evidence standard is met.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice|Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice]]:** The justice system ought to be first about protecting victims and the public and giving offenders what they deserve.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Victims' Rights|Victims' Rights]]:** I hold that offenders lose their rights when they violate others'.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police Power and Accountability|Police Power and Accountability]]:** Police should get more money, more officers, and more respect, but officers who break the law should be punished more harshly than ordinary people.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police and Law Enforcement|Police and Law Enforcement]]:** Body cameras should be required everywhere, the worst crime areas should get militarized enforcement under zero tolerance, and theft and burglary should be treated severely.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Drug Policy|Drug Policy]]:** Using drugs privately should be legal, but not while a minor is present or while impaired around one.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Border Security and Immigration Enforcement|Border Security and Immigration Enforcement]]:** Illegal immigrants should be removed by force, the border ought to be heavily fortified, and lethal force should be allowed when necessary.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Terrorism and Domestic Extremism]]:** Domestic terrorism should be treated as a serious threat.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Political Violence]]:** The 2020 riots were left-wing political violence that terrorism counts leave out, and I condemn both sides' violence equally.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Race and Crime]]:** Black Americans commit violent crime at much higher rates than white Americans (arrest and victim surveys).
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Gun Crime and Violence]]:** Gang and high-crime areas should get heavy, hot-spot policing and fast, harsh crackdowns, and high-rate violent offenders should be locked up, with full due process.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Domestic Violence|Domestic Violence]]:** Extreme violence, intentional neglect, beating without drawing blood, and withholding food, water, or shelter as punishment against a spouse or family member should be capital if they cause lasting injury.

## What a critic must reject

Every position here is meant to follow from the premises below plus the evidence. To reject a conclusion, a critic has to show that it doesn't follow, or reject one of these premises and say which. Each premise links to its fullest defense.

- **Axiom 7, status:** "I don't claim this as objective moral fact. It's a coherent system I choose to endorse and want enforced." Every legal order enforces norms on people who reject them, and a realist or a theist enforces the same rules under another label ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^imposing-license|Imposing it]]; [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^realist-content|Realists]]; [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^divine-command|Divine command]]).
- **Axiom 1:** "subjective experience (first-order phenomenal consciousness) shall serve as the basis for moral status and the possession of negative rights." Nothing can matter to a being that experiences nothing, so experience is the precondition for anything mattering to anyone ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^sentience-basis|Why sentience]]).
- **Axiom 2:** "Any being, biological or artificial, that possesses first-order subjective experience is to be treated as a rights-bearer." Every narrower line, by species, contract, intelligence, or substrate, leaves out beings that can be harmed, infants included, for reasons that have nothing to do with harm ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^contract-egoism|Contract, egoism, substrate]]).
- **Axiom 3:** "A being on an undisturbed developmental trajectory toward sentience ... shall be granted significant moral weight proportional to the robustness of that trajectory." Left undisturbed, it becomes a being that can be harmed, and a voluntary act of procreation creates responsibility for it ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Normative Ethics|Normative Ethics]]; [[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Abortion and Bioethics|Abortion]]).
- **Axiom 4:** "A being that once possessed sentience retains residual moral consideration." Known wishes don't lapse when a person can no longer voice them, as advance directives already recognize ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#End-of-Life Care and Lost Sentience|End-of-life care]]).
- **Axiom 5:** "Practical determination of sentience shall follow the best available scientific consensus." Who can suffer is a question for evidence, not opinion, and where the evidence is unclear the rule leans toward consistency with the axioms, not toward convenience ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Sentience Determination|Sentience Determination]]).
- **Axiom 6:** "strong negative rights against interference and harm," working as "side-constraints" that give way only at "a clear threshold of rights violation by the agent." No total feels the suffering of the one sacrificed, and aggregation applied consistently would kill one patient to save five ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^side-constraints|Side-constraints]]). Rights are reciprocal, so a deliberate violator can't claim the protection he violated ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^forfeiture-basis|Forfeiture]]).
- **Negative rights only:** a positive right to goods compels someone else to provide them, which is itself interference ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^negative-only|Negative rights]]).
- **Axiom 7, consistency:** "All moral and political rules within this system must remain consistent with the prescribed primacy of subjective experience." A rule anyone demands for themselves binds them for everyone in the same position (Hare), so every hard rule has to follow from the axioms and the evidence ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^coherence-rules|Coherence and hard rules]]).
- **Standard of proof:** proof means testable, repeatable evidence or reasoning that others can check, for moral and factual claims alike ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Meta-Ethics|Meta-Ethics]]).

## Apparent tensions and my resolutions

Each of these can look like a contradiction. Here is how each one follows from my framework.

1. **Liberty versus the office bars and the mosque cap.** The bars are prophylaxis, not punishment. They protect the rights order from ideologies that are hostile to it. No one would be fined, jailed, or silenced for belief. Only the four named ideologies meet the test, and adherence is defined by something checkable, never by ancestry alone ([[4. Religion & Secularism#Oath of Office and Perjury|Oath of Office and Perjury]]). The office bar withholds a power no one has a right to, and the building cap falls on institutions that propagate the program, while existing buildings and private worship stay. Ordinary religious and political disagreement fails the four-part test ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^ideology-line|Where the line is]]).
2. **Property versus the two exceptions.** The exceptions follow from forfeiture (animal-industry firms violated rights) and from the excluded-ideology rules. They are not ad hoc breaks in the property right. Banning a harmful use without compensation is settled law, and the Fourteenth Amendment has already voided claims to property in sentient beings ([[9. Energy & Environment#^no-compensation|Compensation]]).
3. **A minimal state versus the sanctuary system and predator removal.** Protecting rights is the state's core duty. The sanctuary system and predator removal are part of that job, not an expansion of it. "Minimal" means minimal in purpose, not in size, and both are built to shrink: no animal is bred into the sanctuaries, and predator numbers fall through fertility control. The full answer is in [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Purpose of the State|Purpose of the State]].
4. **Legal drug use versus capital punishment for large-scale distribution.** Personal use affects only the person using. Large-scale distribution puts doses of unknown strength, often cut with fentanyl, into the hands of many people who cannot know what they are taking, which is why I believe it should be a capital crime. Sharing a known, tested dose with a consenting adult is not capital, because the recipient knows the risk he accepts. Where each line sits, and what would move it, is in [[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Drug Policy|Drug Policy]].
5. **A 12-week rights claim versus a ban from conception.** Before 12 weeks a fetus on its way to sentience already carries significant moral weight (Axiom 3), and where science is unclear I err on the side of consistency with my axioms (Axiom 5). The ban from conception rests on both, and it is also a safeguard against documented drift past 12 weeks: Denmark, Norway, France, and Massachusetts Chapter 188. Before 12 weeks the ban protects no right of the embryo. It rests on that moral weight and on the responsibility created by voluntary procreation, which is why a pregnancy from rape is treated differently ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Normative Ethics|Normative Ethics]]; [[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Abortion and Bioethics|Abortion and Bioethics]]).
6. **Values, not ethnicity, versus group statistics.** Group statistics never become verdicts on individuals; each person is judged on their own values and conduct. Group data may set defaults, such as priors, screening thresholds, and where police go, but it never decides anyone's guilt, punishment, or legal status, and every default can be rebutted by the person's own record. The operating rule, with worked cases, is in [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Group Statistics and Individual Judgment|Group Statistics and Individual Judgment]]. How the immigration presumption fits that rule is in [[4. Religion & Secularism#^group-presumption|Group presumption and individual vetting]]. Stereotypes and prejudice aren't necessarily bad as long as they don't lead to rights violations. Pattern recognition is reasonable even when the person turns out to be harmless, such as wariness of a hooded Black man at night or a suspicious-looking Muslim on a plane. Statistics showing much higher criminality among Black Americans shouldn't be denied either. Pretending nothing is going on isn't virtuous, it's stupid. Wariness of that kind is private caution, which takes no one's rights; a state default may never rest on race ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^group-statistics-rule|rules 4 and 6]]). Critics who use group statistics to prove discrimination already accept them as evidence ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^group-stats-radioactive|Group statistics]]).
7. **Anti-realism versus strong moral rankings.** Every ranking runs on my own prescribed scale, which is the stated basis of everything I write here. Why a prescribed system can still be enforced is answered in [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Meta-Ethics|Meta-Ethics]]. A realist would enforce the same rules under a different label, and each hard rule has to follow from the axioms and the evidence ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^realist-content|Realists]]; [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^coherence-rules|Coherence]]).
8. **Conservation versus "no wilderness."** Wilderness is obsolete. It is full of animal-rights violations and should be engineered by humankind. Preserved habitat stays, but it is managed against predation rather than left wild. Conservation in my sense protects the animals living in a habitat, not the predation in it. The methods are stated: captive predator lines end, non-threatening predators go to sanctuary, herbivorization is pursued where the science works, and every step is held to the stop rule ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Hunting, Fishing, Pets, and Zoos|Hunting, Fishing, Pets, and Zoos]]; [[9. Energy & Environment#^status-quo-burden|Burden of proof]]).
9. **Backing Trump versus opposing his oversteps.** Supporting the administration's direction while opposing its oversteps is ordinary support with limits. Each action is graded on its own, from Aligned to Opposed, and the running totals show both the direction and the dissents ([[12. Current Administration#Summary of Grades|Summary of Grades]]).
10. **Shared ground with the Greens.** The Greens are right only on animal rights. They're wrong on economics and on the other social issues. Even the Greens offer reforms, such as extending the Animal Welfare Act and replacing factory farms, not the day-one end of animal agriculture I want, so a Green vote would buy reform on animals at the cost of opposition on nearly everything else ([[11. Modern Political Parties#Green Party|Green Party]]).
11. **Ranked choice and initiatives inside a restricted vote.** How people vote is separate from how many vote. I believe the electorate should be drastically reduced, and those who qualify should vote by the best method. Where the franchise criteria come from, and what would change them, is in [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise|Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise]].

12. **Feasibility versus accepted costs.** Some of my positions can't be carried out today, and some carry real costs I accept rather than deny. Not possible today: cultivated meat at the scale and price of meat, fertility control for wild predators, and any gene drive in a wild vertebrate ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Hunting, Fishing, Pets, and Zoos|What works now]]); and, without constitutional amendment or new Supreme Court rulings, the qualified franchise, mandatory death sentences, and public execution ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise|Qualified Franchise]], [[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Mandatory Death Penalty]]). Costs I accept: scarce and costlier foods during the switch, lost jobs concentrated in farming counties, and the Sanctuary Levy ([[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Sanctuary Plan]]); ecological risk from removing predators, held to the rule that no step causes more suffering than it prevents ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Wild-Animal Predation|Wild-Animal Predation]]); and extradition refusals and diplomatic friction ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]); and, if AI displaced nearly all work quickly, taxing capital beyond what "minimal" means today to fund the floor ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology#AI and Automation|AI and Automation]]). One gap I haven't closed: what enforcing the food ban will cost in inspectors, customs checks, and prosecutions has no estimate yet, and I flag it as Needs research ([[9. Energy & Environment#Enforcement|Enforcement]]). Where a tool doesn't exist yet, the duty uses the tools that do. Where a cost is real, I name it and say why the rights at stake outweigh it. Why I accept these risks at all: the status quo kills billions of land animals and over a trillion fish every year, so the burden of proof falls on keeping it, not on ending it ([[9. Energy & Environment#^status-quo-burden|Burden of proof]]).

    Each hard position names its own cost where I argue it:
    - **Death where the victim lived:** a proven offender who would never have offended again is executed too ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#^victim-lived|Why death where the victim lived]]).
    - **Finality:** an error built on evidence that looked solid at trial, and that no new objective evidence exposes, will not be caught ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#^finality-cost|New evidence after the verdict]]).
    - **Compelled labor:** the work is hard and unpaid ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#^labor-cost|Compelled labor]]).
    - **Qualified franchise:** a capable citizen who skips the test goes unheard by choice ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^license-cost|Why a license to vote]]).
    - **Office bar:** open adherents lose authority roles they could otherwise hold ([[4. Religion & Secularism#^office-bar-cost|The office bar and free speech]]).
    - **Mosque cap:** peaceful congregations cannot build new places of worship ([[4. Religion & Secularism#^mosque-cap-cost|Why cap mosques]]).
    - **Group presumption:** some applicants who would have been fine are turned away ([[4. Religion & Secularism#^group-presumption|Group presumption]]).
    - **No compensation:** shareholders, including fund savers, lose that value ([[9. Energy & Environment#^no-comp-cost|No compensation]]).
    - **Opt-in welfare:** some people get less from the state than today's programs give ([[2. Political and Social Character#^opt-in-cost|Opt-in welfare]]).
    - **Research on offenders:** some research will be slower or will not happen ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology#^research-cost|Research capacity]]).
    - **Predator removal:** less carrion for scavengers, disease spread without predators, and herbivore overshoot, each watched under the stop rule ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#^predation-risks|Risks I accept and watch]]).

13. **Treaty law versus my rules.** Exiting the ICCPR, possibly denouncing the Convention Against Torture, compelled labor for prisoners of war beyond Geneva's limits, and West Bank removals that conflict with Geneva IV Art. 49 and the Rome Statute all depart from treaties. Treaty law is not my moral standard: a rule is not right or wrong because a convention forbids it, any more than a punishment is wrong because other democracies dropped it ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#^not-moral-standard|the same test as the death penalty]]). The standard is forfeiture by the person's own conduct, and every departure is individual, decided by a court or military tribunal, never en masse or by identity ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#^hostile-forfeit|Hostile conduct, removal, and entry]]). The United States should leave through its own constitutional procedure, because no nation is bound forever to a foreign committee's reading of a treaty it chose to join ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#^treaty-exit|Treaty exits]]). I acknowledge that Article 49's text bars individual transfers too, so this answers the charge of ethnic cleansing, not the treaty.
14. **Due process versus emergency on-scene sentencing.** Ordinary times get the 30-day capital trial and a unanimous jury. On-scene sentencing should exist only inside a declared emergency over riots or violent crime that local policing can't contain, and the emergency expires on a fixed date unless the legislature renews it. It speeds up the process, not the standard of proof: the officer must be certain to the full capital standard, every case is recorded on body camera, a civilian court reviews it afterward, and an officer whose sentence the record doesn't support is charged as for murder. That is stricter than today's law, where *Tennessee v. Garner* already allows deadly force on probable cause ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^emergency-capital|Emergency sentencing]]).
15. **Double jeopardy versus a capital retrial after nullification.** I would allow one retrial, only after a finding of nullification, only in mandatory-death-penalty cases, and before a different jury held to the same unanimity rule and capital standard. Successive trials for the same act already exist: under the dual-sovereignty doctrine, which the Supreme Court declined to overturn in *Gamble v. United States* (2019), a state and the federal government may each prosecute the same conduct. I acknowledge this departs from the Fifth Amendment rule that an acquittal is final ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Role of Juries|Role of Juries]]).
16. **Causal liability versus the secret ballot.** Reparations are owed only by those who caused the harm. Lawmakers' votes and officials' enforcement acts are on the public record, so the people who passed or carried out a policy can be held liable now. Voters can't be, because the secret ballot records no one's vote, and I keep the secret ballot ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Race and Diversity|Race and Diversity]]).
17. **Free association versus birth-sex bathroom rules for private businesses.** It is a mandate, and it rests on protection and privacy in multi-person rooms where people undress. It is not a new kind of rule: federal workplace law already requires toilet rooms "separate for each sex" in all places of employment (29 CFR 1910.141). Single-occupancy lockable rooms are open to anyone, so no one is left without access ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Gender Identity in Schools and Institutions|Gender Identity]]).
