## 1. Summary label set

**In one sentence:** I am a **sentientist threshold deontologist whose rights theory runs on forfeiture**. I want a **qualified-franchise epistocratic republic**, which is a modern Aristotelian polity. That republic would have:

- a **minimal, opt-in fiscal state**;
- a **maximal, forfeiture-based penal code**;
- **values-based civilizational nationalism with secular anti-Islam assimilationism**;
- **hawkish America-First realism**;
- a **transhumanist program to abolish animal agriculture and reorder nature against predation**.

I vote **Republican as the best directional fit**.

**In a few words:** *Sentientist epistocratic national-individualist*. In full: *sentientist, negative-rights, forfeiture-retributivist, epistocratic-republican, civilizational-nationalist, free-market, transhumanist-abolitionist*.

**In more detail:**

- **Metaethics and rights.** I am a moral anti-realist of the constructivist-prescriptivist kind (Hume plus Hare). On that base I build a threshold deontology of negative rights, using one prescribed criterion: first-order sentience, in any substrate. I believe rights act as Nozickian side-constraints, and that they are lost only through forfeiture (Wellman). Forfeiture runs the rest of my system. I believe there should be:
  - a mandatory death penalty for a long list of crimes;
  - a three-mark rule that forfeits the right to life;
  - public executions;
  - an experimentation track for the condemned, in place of animal testing;
  - organ use from condemned offenders;
  - abroad, loss of civilian protection for people who commit hostile acts.
- **Regime.** I want a constitutional republic with a voter license test and a net-taxpayer franchise. That is an Aristotelian polity or timocracy, close to Brennan's epistocracy. I believe ranked-choice voting should be required everywhere.
- **The state.** I lean minarchist. I want a flat tax, an earnings-only floor in the style of a negative income tax, opt-in welfare, Social Security, Medicare, and subsidies, and no minimum wage. I believe the state should be heavily coercive at the border and against crime.
- **Excluded ideologies.** I name four: Nazism, communism, socialism, and Islam. I believe their adherents should be barred from sworn authority roles and the buildings that promote them capped, with no speech penalty.
- **Culture.** I am a secular agnostic atheist who prefers traditional family life without mandating it. I am gender-critical, yet I support same-sex marriage and anti-discrimination law. I am colorblind and against DEI, and near-absolutist on speech. I would legalize private vice, but I believe hard-drug supply should be a capital crime. I treat pattern recognition as reasonable: I acknowledge group statistics, but I believe each person should be judged on their own conduct.
- **Abroad.** My view is Jacksonian-style: pro-Israel, regime change in Iran by force, pro-Ukraine, sovereigntist, and no nation-building. I believe Congress must authorize a war within 30 days.
- **Nature and technology.** I am an eco-modernist and transhumanist. I would end animal agriculture, fishing, and animal testing on day one, and pursue Pearce-style herbivorization of predators.
- **Parties.** I am a Republican-leaning independent who backs the Trump administration overall. Of the 87 actions I grade in [[12. Current Administration|S12]], 52 point toward my view and 20 point away. My main dissents are on animals, tariffs, entitlements, vouchers, war-powers process, and due process.

## 2. Axis by axis

Each axis gives my label, related labels, why the label fits, and where I depart from the standard version of that view. Section references ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]] to [[13. News Commentary|S13]]) link to the full positions.

### 2.1 Metaethics

**Label:** Constructivist-prescriptivist moral anti-realism.

**Related labels:** Humean constructivism plus Harean universal prescriptivism, with an expressivist element. Coherentist prescriptivism.

**Why:** [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]] Meta-Ethics: "I claim no objective moral truth. I prescribe a sentience-centered system because it is the most coherent and stably consistent framework available." I describe my view as a hybrid: "constructivist in the Humean sense… prescriptivist in form, in the tradition of R. M. Hare… an expressive element." In [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]] I also:

- give three supporting arguments: disagreement, Mackie's queerness argument, and Street and Joyce's evolutionary debunking;
- answer the realist replies, companions-in-guilt and self-debunking, in full;
- state that those who reject my system "are still bound by its enforcement" ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]], Axiom 7: "a coherent system I choose to endorse and want enforced").

**Where I depart from the standard version:** Most anti-realists hold back from strong first-order moral confidence. I draw hard verdicts and treat enforcement as part of my system's authority. Mine is a *legislative* or *prescriptive* anti-realism, not a relativist one.

### 2.2 Normative ethics

**Label:** Threshold sentientist deontology: negative-rights side-constraints plus forfeiture.

**Related labels:** "Prescriptive Threshold Sentientist Deontology (Negative-rights focused)," the name I give it in [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]]. A two-level view: deontology for politics and Stoic eudaimonism for personal character.

**Why:** [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]] Axioms 1-7. I believe rights are side-constraints, "not goals to maximize," and that the numbers never license harming an innocent ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Conflicts Between Rights-Holders|S1 Conflicts Between Rights-Holders]]). Rights give way only when an agent crosses the Axiom 6 threshold: violating rights by force or fraud, or directly imposing severe non-consensual risk. "I'm a threshold deontologist, not a utilitarian" ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]]). In [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]] I add: "My framework doesn't rest on Kantian dignity. It rests on negative rights, which an agent forfeits by violating them in others."

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- **The threshold.** In standard threshold deontology, a threshold of bad *consequences* overrides the constraints (Moore). In my view the threshold is the *agent's own violation*, so mine is a forfeiture theory more than a catastrophe-threshold theory.
- **Proportionality.** I believe it should be applied once, at the forfeiture decision: a court finds, under due process and at the capital evidentiary standard, that the threshold was crossed ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#General Rules of Forfeiture|S1 General Rules of Forfeiture]]; [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]]). Once rights are forfeit, "the sentence carried out within them is not a further injustice." The experimentation track works the same way: the offender lost those protections by violating another's rights. I believe harm outside the sentence, such as guard abuse unrelated to any study, should still be punished.
- **Duties to sustain others.** I believe these arise only from voluntary acts, as in the procreation-responsibility basis for my abortion position.

### 2.3 Moral status and animal ethics

**Label:** Sentientism that is substrate-neutral and graded, with equal rights above a class threshold.

**Related labels:** Abolitionist animal rights. Wild-animal-suffering interventionism. Predation abolitionism.

**Why:**

- **Basis.** [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]] Axioms 1-2. I believe moral status rests on first-order phenomenal consciousness, "biological or artificial," with no self-awareness requirement.
- **Sentience classes.** I believe they should be set in statute by an expert board and revised only for acts committed after a revision. The high class covers mammals, birds, cephalopods, and decapods, including rats, mice, and pigeons. Fish and shrimp should be placed species by species. Bivalves and insects should sit in a precautionary tier, with a small fine.
- **Pests.** I believe pest status should never demote a species, and lethal control should be allowed only for a defined infestation of a dwelling, food store, or medical facility.
- **Killing.** I believe killing a high-class animal is murder and should be on the capital track. My view is that hunting is murder, with no subsistence or Indigenous exception.
- **Day-one bans.** I believe animal agriculture, fishing, aquaculture, breeding mills, and animal testing should be banned on day one, under the [[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Sanctuary Plan]] ([[9. Energy & Environment|S9]]).
- **Ex post facto.** I believe no one should be punished for killings before the ban ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]]).
- **Predation.** I believe it is a rights violation that the U.S. government should stop within its borders, in phases, with no step that causes more suffering than it prevents ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]], [[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]]).

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- Sentientism is usually utilitarian (Singer). Mine is rights-based.
- I use sentience, not Regan's "subject-of-a-life."
- Regan denied any duty to stop predation. I believe there is one.
- Francione-style abolitionists are nonviolent and anti-carceral. I believe animal killing belongs on the capital track.
- Above the class threshold, I believe the right to life is equal. In [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]] I make the infant-and-philosopher analogy: "a pig's killing is sentenced like a human's."
- I believe euthanasia that breaks the rules should be graded on a sliding scale, not treated as automatically capital ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]]).

### 2.4 Political philosophy and regime type

**Label:** Epistocratic constitutional republic with a qualified franchise.

**Related labels:** A modern **Aristotelian polity** (politeia) or **timocracy**. Limited democracy. Minarchist-leaning constitutionalism. A form of **militant democracy**, Karl Loewenstein's term, for the excluded-ideologies amendment.

**Why:**

- **Franchise ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties|S3]]).** I believe in a stake qualification (net taxpayers for the prior year, "close to what Aristotle called timocracy") plus a competence qualification (a voter license test set by a bipartisan commission, with pass rates by group published).
- **Elections and offices.** I believe ranked-choice voting should be required by amendment, and the Electoral College kept, with electors chosen by ranked choice. I believe there should be no term limits, and three-year cycles. Parties are private associations, and I believe top-two primaries should be banned. Rules for deceptive candidacies should be fixed before the election.
- **Direct democracy.** I would create a national initiative for qualified voters only (1% of them for a statute, 2% for an amendment).
- **Money.** I believe contributions to candidates should come from individuals only, while independent speech stays free.
- **Other rules.** I believe felons should not vote, and there should be a 60-day restraint on federal enforcement before an election.
- **Purpose.** In my view the state exists for "protection of negative rights and the security of the political community" ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]], citing Nozick).
- **Party comparison.** In [[11. Modern Political Parties|S11]] I call Switzerland the closest to "my preference for decentralized power under citizen control."

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- Brennan's epistocracy usually comes with cosmopolitan liberalism. Mine comes with civilizational nationalism.
- My stake test is fiscal, not about property or military service.
- I keep Swiss-style initiatives inside an epistocracy.
- Militant democracies such as Germany ban parties. I would ban no party and no speech. I believe adherents should be barred from *sworn authority roles* and the *buildings* that promote the ideologies capped, and I name one religion among them.

### 2.5 Rights theory

**Label:** Negative-rights side-constraint theory with forfeiture.

**Related labels:** Nozickian libertarian rights with a floor justified by security. Consent-based fiscal voluntarism ("opt-in").

**Why:**

- **Positive rights rejected.** [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]]: "I reject positive rights." I believe the floor is "not a right anyone can claim" but part of the state's security role. It should cover emergency shelter and emergency medical care, about \$10,000 per child for K-12, an earnings-only top-up, and a last-resort allowance.
- **Property.** [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties|S3]]: "once ownership is properly established, it should last," and I believe regulatory takings should be compensated.
- **Opt-in everywhere.** I believe these should be opt-in: welfare, Social Security, Medicare, the public health plan, subsidies, data trading, facial recognition, social credit, organ donation, and vaccines.
- **The two property exceptions** rest on forfeiture and on the excluded-ideology rules. [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Property Rights|S3 Property Rights]] says: "Two cases fall outside that protection: one is barred by amendment, and in the other the owners forfeited it."
- **The sanctuary seizure** is the uncompensated conversion or liquidation of animal-industry firms. [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]], [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties|S3]], [[9. Energy & Environment|S9]], and [[Where I Stand]] ground it in forfeiture: "animal-industry firms forfeited ordinary property protections by using their assets to violate animals' rights." [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties|S3]] calls it "a one-time forfeiture, not a break in the property right." The Sanctuary Levy of no more than 3% is separate. In [[9. Energy & Environment|S9]] I call it "a time-limited tax to fund sanctuary care, and I accept that cost."
- **The building bar.** Under my excluded-ideologies amendment, I believe places that promote Nazism, communism, socialism, or Islam should not be built, rebuilt, expanded, or relocated, and existing ones should be capped ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties|S3]]).

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- Strict Nozickians accept no compelled floor. I would fund one through minimal taxes.
- I treat both property exceptions as following from my own premises: firms whose assets were used to violate animals' rights, and institutions organized to subvert the rights order. A standard libertarian would not.
- **Defaults.** I would use opt-out default enrollment only where a person protects themselves, as with private disability insurance, retirement accounts, and index accounts ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance|S5]], [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]]). That is libertarian paternalism, the "nudge" approach.

### 2.6 Economics

**Label:** Flat-tax, free-market classical liberalism with an opt-in welfare state and a floor modeled on a negative income tax.

**Related labels:** Minarchist-leaning fiscal conservatism. Friedmanite: an NIT-style top-up and a rule-based Fed. Pronatalist family-credit conservatism. Libertarian-paternalist defaults.

**Why ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance|S5]], [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]]):**

- **Taxes.** I believe in a flat 20% income tax with a zero bracket of about \$42k single and \$84k joint, a 2-point transition surcharge through 2041, and 15% on capital income, with carryover basis instead of an estate tax. I believe there should be no VAT, sales tax, estate tax, or wealth tax at any level of government, and full expensing. I accept, on principle, that a flat tax cuts most for the rich.
- **Welfare.** I believe the earnings-only top-up should replace the EITC and CTC, with a last-resort allowance. SNAP, farm payments, and non-emergency Medicaid should be wound down, with phase-outs that honor current recipients. Non-citizens should be ineligible.
- **Social Security and Medicare.** I believe they should be voluntary for new entrants and kept in full for anyone within 10 years of full retirement age. The transition should be paid for by a levy capped at 16%.
- **Labor.** I believe in no minimum wage, state minimums included; voluntary unions; and licensing only against real harm.
- **Antitrust.** "Big is not illegal."
- **Budget.** I believe in a 30% cut to nondefense spending, a debt brake, a balanced budget by 2050, and ending NGO grants, with contracts only. For the civil service, I believe in merit hiring, policy roles at the pleasure of elected officials, and fast removal with stated reasons.
- **Fed.** I believe in a single price-stability rule, no bailouts, competing currencies, and no gold standard, with governors removable only for cause.
- **Trade.** I believe trade should be reciprocal. Tariffs are "good in theory, questionable in practice," and I believe they belong to Congress. I believe the Jones Act mandate should go.
- **Education.** I believe about \$10,000 per child should follow the child to *public or charter* schools, with no vouchers or ESAs and a \$1,000 homeschool credit. I would abolish the federal Department of Education, require gifted tracks, and end new federal student loans.
- **Family.** I believe in a child credit that doubles from the third child, a birth credit for third and later children, and income-tax exemptions for mothers of three (half) and four (full). I also believe in housing deregulation, no paid-leave mandate, and unrestricted IVF.
- **AI displacement ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]]).** I believe in no UBI and no citizen dividend. Every adult should be default-enrolled in a private index account. A BLS trigger should raise the tax on capital income to the 20% flat rate, then up to a 25% ceiling, with each step reversing on recovery. I say plainly that fast, near-total displacement would force a choice beyond a "minimal" state, and that my choice then would be a large income funded by taxing capital.

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- From standard conservatism: no vouchers, no broad tariffs, no protection for entitlements, no farm subsidies.
- From libertarianism: I would keep a public K-12 guarantee, a voluntary public health plan, natalist credits, and a trigger that can raise taxes on capital under measured displacement.
- From economic nationalism: I prefer tax and regulatory relief to tariffs and mandates.
- My opt-in-taxpayer funding device ("subsidies only from taxpayers who choose to chip in") is close to my own invention.

### 2.7 Criminal justice and the scope of capital punishment

**Label:** Hard retributivism through rights forfeiture, or penal maximalism.

**Related labels:** Punitive civil libertarian: a maximal penalty scale with strict procedural safeguards. Close to Singapore's and Bukele's approaches ([[11. Modern Political Parties|S11]], [[13. News Commentary|S13]]).

**Why ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security|S6]], [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties|S3]], [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]]):**

- **Mandatory death.** I believe there should be a mandatory death penalty for:
  - murder, including attempted murder and knowingly creating extreme risk;
  - violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and trafficking;
  - selling or distributing hard drugs;
  - domestic violence or cruelty that causes lasting injury;
  - killing a high-sentience animal.
- **The victim's choice.** I believe the victim should choose between execution and the experimentation track. Where the victim is dead, the judge should have to choose and should not be allowed to refuse.
- **Three marks.** I believe three marks should forfeit the right to life, which can reach repeat thieves. The victim should choose execution, experimentation, or life with labor.
- **Process.** I believe in:
  - 30-day capital trials and same-day execution, with no death row;
  - public execution by hanging or gunshot;
  - no fixed age of criminal responsibility, with a maturity assessment before a capital sentence;
  - no sentence reduction for mental competence;
  - confinement for insanity, then execution as defense against a permanent threat after three years without progress;
  - compelled hard labor for every sentence, and no idle incarceration;
  - day fines scaled to income and wealth, with CEOs personally liable.
- **Justification.** I ground it in desert, incapacitation, and forfeiture, not deterrence.
- **Emergencies ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|S3]], [[6. Criminal Justice & Security|S6]]).** I believe that inside a declared, time-limited emergency, El Salvador-style sweeps should be allowed, and so should on-scene sentencing by police or military. The capital standard, body cameras, and later review would still apply, and an officer whose sentence is not supported would be charged with murder.

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- Unlike tough-on-crime conservatives, I would abolish qualified immunity and hold police to a higher liability standard; release defendants before trial by default and abolish money bail; require unanimous 12-person juries in civil and criminal cases; end civil forfeiture without conviction; and compensate the wrongly convicted, paid by the prosecuting authority.
- I would set an "objective evidence" capital standard: video or physical proof, not testimony alone.
- My view departs from *Roper*, *Miller*, *Woodson*, *Atkins*, *Ford*, *Coker*, and *Kennedy*. I believe the 8th Amendment should be amended or abolished.
- My capital scope is wider than any U.S. tradition.

### 2.8 Social and cultural positions

**Label:** Secular, gender-critical cultural traditionalist ("preference, not mandate") with classical liberal carve-outs for personal liberty.

**Related labels:** Classical liberal-conservative fusionism on culture. Colorblind meritocrat. Free-speech near-absolutist.

**Why, by topic:**

- **Abortion.** I believe the rights claim starts at 12 weeks, and elective abortion should be banned from conception by amendment, as a safeguard against drift. There should be rape and life-of-mother exceptions. Assisted death should be allowed for competent adults, with safeguards ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]], [[Glossary]], [[13. News Commentary|S13]] Chapter 188).
- **Sex and gender.** My view is:
  - "A man is an adult human male and a woman is an adult human female."
  - Transgenderism is "a mental illness."
  - Adults should be free to transition privately; minors should get no treatment.
  - Bathrooms (including private businesses), sports, ID, and prisons should go by birth sex.
  - There should be no pronoun mandates.
  - Coercive conversion methods should be banned; exploratory therapy should be allowed.
  - Trans people should be able to serve in the military under ordinary medical standards ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]]).
- **Family.** I believe traditional roles are best but should never be mandated. Same-sex civil marriage should be legal, and there should be no default custody rule. Incest between blood relatives should be banned as a precaution. The age of consent should be 18, with a close-in-age exception ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security|S6]], [[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]]).
- **Speech.** I believe only true threats and incitement should be punishable. Hate speech should be legal, and private platforms should be free to moderate. There should be no heckler's veto: the state should protect the event. Deepfakes are speech ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]], [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology|S10]], [[13. News Commentary|S13]]).
- **Religion.** I believe church and state should be strictly separated, churches should be taxed, there should be no school prayer, and ritual slaughter should be banned for every faith. Moderate Christianity and Western heritage are "good"; Islam is "the most toxic, problematic major religion on earth by a lot." Under the excluded-ideologies amendment I propose, no Muslims should hold sworn authority roles, no new mosques should be built, and existing mosques should be capped. My stated aim is for the ideologies to "die out over time" through environment, not force ([[4. Religion & Secularism|S4]]).
- **Vice and minors.** I believe marijuana should be legal outright, and I would vote No on repealing Massachusetts retail sales ([[13. News Commentary|S13]]). Hard drugs should be legal for private use, but supply should be capital, with no commercial market. Prostitution should be licensed and gambling legal. Adult porn should be legal, but minors' access banned. There should be no social media under 16, and parental opt-in for ages 16-18 ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]]).
- **Guns.** I believe in qualified ownership, with background checks and red-flag laws by court order ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]]).

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- Unlike social conservatives, I am secular, against Christian law, for same-sex marriage, and for legalizing vice.
- Unlike liberal secularists, I would put civic disabilities on Islam. This is a much more forceful version of the "liberal counter-jihad" pattern.
- I describe the disabilities as "not a penalty": I believe no one should be fined, jailed, or silenced for belief.

### 2.9 Race and group statistics

**Label:** Individualist colorblind meritocracy with pattern realism.

**Related labels:** "Judged as an individual, by the same law." Anti-DEI equal treatment, applied in either direction.

**Why:**

- **My position.** Group statistics never become verdicts on individuals. Each person should be judged on their own values and conduct. Stereotypes and prejudice aren't necessarily bad as long as they don't lead to rights violations. Pattern recognition is reasonable even when the person turns out to be harmless. Examples: wariness of a hooded Black man at night, or of a suspicious-looking Muslim on a plane. Group statistics showing much higher criminality among Black Americans shouldn't be denied: "pretending nothing is going on isn't virtuous, it's stupid."
- **The evidence.** [[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Race and Violent Crime|S6]] presents BJS data under a settled/contested/unresolved framing. "Every offender should be judged as an individual, by the same law." "No average tells anything about an individual." Whether the ability gap is genetic is "unresolved," and I lay out both hereditarian and environmental research.
- **The law.** In [[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]] I would allow police profiling alongside random checks. I believe employers, landlords, and insurers should *not* be allowed to use group statistics, except actuarial sex facts. I believe in no quotas or DEI, repealing disparate-impact liability, and requiring tests in selective admissions. I would charge reparations only to voters of the responsible government, as a principle, with the secret ballot kept. [[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]]: "race doesn't matter in itself; culture and assimilation do."

**Where I depart from the standard version:** Unlike standard colorblind conservatives, I welcome research on group differences and say outright that pattern recognition is reasonable. Unlike race-realist movements, I would bar private actors from deciding by group, and I make the individual the only unit of verdict.

### 2.10 Immigration

**Label:** Values-selective restrictionism, or civilizational assimilationism.

**Related labels:** Civic, non-ethnic civilizational nationalism. Compatibility-vetted immigration.

**Why ([[2. Political and Social Character|S2]], [[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations|S8]]):**

- **Western values.** In my view "Western" is "a set of values, not an ethnicity." I believe fit with Western values should come first and labor needs second.
- **Muslim applicants.** I believe being Muslim should be a rebuttable presumption against admission. It could be overcome only by a five-part verified-conduct test, and passing that test would not lift the office bar.
- **The Questionable Compatibility list.** I believe in:
  - **hard entry bans** by nationality where a regime or dominant faction backs genocidal attacks, or where 5% or more openly support them. My list: the West Bank and Gaza, Iran, Yemen, Lebanon, Sudan, Burma, China, Afghanistan, and 16 Arab states, based on ACRPS polling;
  - **a case-by-case, default-no tier**, including Russia, Pakistan, Turkey, Nigeria, Mexico, Venezuela, and Haiti;
  - a published rule for lifting a ban.
- **Enforcement and status.** I believe in lethal force at the border when necessary, removal by force with no amnesty, abolishing sanctuary cities, and expanding ICE, with body cameras. I believe birthright citizenship should end by amendment, going forward only.
- **Green-card and visa holders.** I believe they should be removed after a court finding by clear and convincing evidence, for welfare use, failing the civics test, or any crime above a basic infraction. Refugees should go only to a safe third country. No one should be removed for speech.
- **Terrorism.** I believe it should be punished, never deported in place of punishment. I would amend the Constitution to strip citizenship from dual nationals.
- **Investor visas.** I believe there should be none.

**Where I depart from the standard version:** Unlike ethnonationalists, my test is explicitly about values and open to any ancestry. I call many Latin American families compatible. In [[12. Current Administration|S12]] I fault selecting refugees by ethnic group. Unlike most restrictionists, my approach is religion-specific and puts cohesion above the CBO's fiscal gains.

### 2.11 Foreign policy and defense

**Label:** America First hawkish realism with "selective Western imperialism," my own phrase, defined as defeating and containing threats without governing territory.

**Related labels:** Jacksonian (Walter Russell Mead). Sovereigntist hawk. Interventionism that punishes threats but rejects nation-building.

**Why ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations|S8]], [[12. Current Administration|S12]], [[13. News Commentary|S13]]):**

- **Iran.** I believe the regime and its proxies should be destroyed, sparing the people. The administration is "too soft." I "would vote to authorize" the war, but I insist Congress must authorize any sustained campaign within 30 days.
- **Israel.** I back Israel as the "first line of Western defense." My view is:
  - there should be no two-state solution; Gaza "should likely be dissolved";
  - the West Bank's armed threat should be removed and the territory walled off, and annexation is justified;
  - individuals a court finds hostile by conduct should be forced out;
  - "International law should be disregarded here," and I acknowledge the conflict with Geneva IV Art. 49;
  - civilian protection should be absolute, except for foreign actors who forfeit it by hostile *conduct*.
- **Russia and China.** Russia is an aggressor, and I believe Ukraine should be helped. China is an enemy to the extent it seeks to destabilize the U.S.
- **Alliances and territory.** I support NATO only when it serves U.S. interests and new territory only by consent. I grade Greenland Mixed.
- **Institutions.** I believe in no ICC; exiting the ICCPR and, if needed, denouncing the CAT to protect the experimentation track and new capital offenses; and not being bound by the WHO.
- **Force and law of war.** I believe cartels should be treated as terrorists, including war on governments that refuse to cooperate. Capturing Maduro was right (graded Aligned). POWs found by a tribunal to have fought should be open to compelled labor beyond Geneva's limits.
- **Weapons.** I believe in high military spending, deterrence plus preventive force "when it can be done safely," autonomous weapons only with human judgment kept, and defensive space weapons.

**Where I depart from the standard version:**

- Unlike MAGA restrainers, I am pro-Ukraine and want *more* force on Iran.
- Unlike neoconservatives, I reject nation-building and democracy promotion.
- Unlike classical realists, I use civilizational language, while defining hostility by conduct.
- I am stricter on war powers than the War Powers Resolution: 30 days, versus 60 plus 30.

### 2.12 Environment and energy

**Label:** Market eco-modernism ("reliability first, no mandates, nuclear yes") plus sentientist anti-naturalism.

**Related labels:** Free-market environmentalism, with polluter-pays as a rights violation. Ecomodernist. "Civilize nature."

**Why ([[9. Energy & Environment|S9]], [[7. Cultural & Social Issues|S7]], [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework|S1]]):**

- **Climate.** It is real and almost entirely human-caused (IPCC), but in my view "not apocalyptic."
- **Transition.** I believe it should be slow and market-driven, free of mandates and forced shutdowns, without a carbon price, and without energy subsidies of any kind unless they are opt-in. I call the rest "theft."
- **Greenhouse gases.** I treat them as pollution above the natural baseline. I believe they should be governed by harm-based limits that tighten if credible tail risk appears.
- **Nuclear.** I believe it should expand, through deregulation.
- **Adaptation.** I believe owners should bear the risk of where they build, and the 18% cap on flood-insurance increases should be removed.

## 4. Apparent tensions and my resolutions

Each of these can look like a contradiction. Here is how each one follows from my framework.

1. **Liberty versus the office bars and the mosque cap.** The bars are prophylaxis, not punishment. They protect the rights order from ideologies that are hostile to it. No one would be fined, jailed, or silenced for belief.
2. **Property versus the two exceptions.** The exceptions follow from forfeiture (animal-industry firms violated rights) and from the excluded-ideology rules. They are not ad hoc breaks in the property right.
3. **A minimal state versus the sanctuary system and predator removal.** Protecting rights is the state's core duty. The sanctuary system and predator removal are part of that job, not an expansion of it.
4. **Legal drug use versus capital punishment for supply.** Personal use affects only the person using. Selling spreads harm to others, which is why I believe it should be a capital crime.
5. **A 12-week rights claim versus a ban from conception.** The ban from conception is a safeguard against documented drift past 12 weeks: Denmark, Norway, France, and Massachusetts Chapter 188.
6. **Values, not ethnicity, versus group statistics.** Group statistics never become verdicts on individuals; each person is judged on their own values and conduct. Stereotypes and prejudice aren't necessarily bad as long as they don't lead to rights violations. Pattern recognition is reasonable even when the person turns out to be harmless, such as wariness of a hooded Black man at night or a suspicious-looking Muslim on a plane. Statistics showing much higher criminality among Black Americans shouldn't be denied either. Pretending nothing is going on isn't virtuous, it's stupid.
7. **Anti-realism versus strong moral rankings.** Every ranking runs on my own prescribed scale, which is the stated basis of my whole site.
8. **Conservation versus "no wilderness."** Wilderness is obsolete. It is full of animal-rights violations and should be engineered by humankind.
9. **Backing Trump versus opposing his oversteps.** Supporting the administration's direction while opposing its oversteps is ordinary support with limits.
10. **Shared ground with the Greens.** The Greens are right only on animal rights. They're wrong on economics and on the other social issues.
11. **Ranked choice and initiatives inside a restricted vote.** How people vote is separate from how many vote. I believe the electorate should be drastically reduced, and those who qualify should vote by the best method.
