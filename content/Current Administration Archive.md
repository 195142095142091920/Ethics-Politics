## Health

#### Withdrawal from the World Health Organization
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

**Status as of Oct 23, 2026:** Resolved.

Executive Order 14155 begins withdrawal of the United States from the World Health Organization.

**Outcome (January 22, 2026):** The withdrawal took effect after the one-year notice period, and U.S. funding, staff and official participation in WHO bodies ended.

The United States ought to take part only in institutions that serve the national interest and not be bound by global bodies ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Global Institutions|Global Institutions]]). The United States ought to cooperate case by case through specific agreements ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#World Health Organization|World Health Organization]]) and I still support cooperation against engineered and natural pandemics ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology#Pandemics and Biosecurity|Pandemics and Biosecurity]]), which the order does not replace.

## Animals, Agriculture, Environment, and Energy

#### Withdrawal from the Paris Agreement
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

**Status as of Oct 23, 2026:** Resolved.

Executive Order 14162 withdraws the United States from the Paris Agreement and related climate commitments.

**Outcome (January 27, 2026):** The withdrawal took effect one year after the United Nations received notice, as Article 28 provides.

I hold that the United States is not beholden to accords that go against the freedom of its people ([[9. Energy & Environment#International Climate Agreements|International Climate Agreements]]). I still support awareness efforts and name animal agriculture as the climate driver whose elimination also protects sentient rights.
