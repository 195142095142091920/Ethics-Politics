## 0-9

**45 CFR 46 (Common Rule)** - The federal regulations protecting human research subjects; Subpart C limits research on prisoners to minimal-risk studies and research that may benefit them. "CFR" is the Code of Federal Regulations. (standard meaning; site S10) ^g-45-cfr-46-common-rule

**60-day restraint** - The Justice Department's practice of avoiding public enforcement steps against candidates close to an election. I believe it should be a fixed written rule, binding in every case, with an exception only for an immediate threat or evidence about to be lost. (site S3, S13) ^g-60-day-restraint

## A

**Abolitionism (animal rights)** - The animal-rights view, linked to Gary Francione and Tom Regan, that animal use should be ended outright rather than made more humane. I share the goal: I believe animal agriculture, fishing, and animal testing should end on day one. Unlike Francione-style abolitionists, who are nonviolent and anti-carceral, I believe animal killing belongs on the capital track. (standard meaning; site S1, S9) ^g-abolitionism-animal-rights

**Abolitionist project (Pearce)** - David Pearce's proposal to use technology to abolish suffering in all sentient life, including suffering caused by predation in the wild. On my site it means the future "reordering of nature against predation and suffering," pursued within U.S. borders in phases, with no step that causes more suffering than it prevents. (standard meaning; site S1, S7, S10) ^g-abolitionist-project-pearce

**Abortion ban from conception** - See [[Glossary#Abortion ban (from conception)|Abortion ban (from conception)]] on the Glossary page. ^g-abortion-ban-from-conception

**ACLED** - The Armed Conflict Location & Event Data project, which tracks protests and political violence; it found campus protests 97% peaceful. (standard meaning) ^g-acled

**ACRPS** - The Arab Center for Research and Policy Studies, whose 2023-2024 survey of 8,000 people in 16 Arab countries my S8 uses to place countries on the entry-ban list. (site S8) ^g-acrps

**Actuarial sex facts** - Sex-based statistics on risk, such as life expectancy or accident rates, used in insurance pricing. I believe this is the one group statistic insurers should be allowed to use. (site S7) ^g-actuarial-sex-facts

**Agnostic atheism** - Not believing in any god (atheism) while holding that the question can't be settled with certainty (agnosticism). The report ties mine to burden-of-proof skepticism: a claim needs testable, repeatable proof before I accept it. (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-agnostic-atheism

**AI preemption** - Preemption means federal law overrides state law. I believe national AI rules should preempt state law, but by statute passed by Congress, not executive order. (site S10) ^g-ai-preemption

**AI, AGI** - AI is artificial intelligence. AGI, artificial general intelligence, is AI that matches or exceeds humans across most tasks. (standard meaning) ^g-ai-agi

**Alaska certification suit** - The Justice Department's October 2026 lawsuit seeking to hold Alaska's U.S. Senate certification. I grade it Directionally opposed. (site S12) ^g-alaska-certification-suit

**Alien Enemies Act** - A 1798 law letting the President detain and remove citizens of a hostile nation during a declared war or invasion. The administration invoked it against the Tren de Aragua gang in March 2025; I grade that Directionally aligned. (standard meaning; site S12) ^g-alien-enemies-act

**AMA and Helsinki codes** - The American Medical Association's Code of Medical Ethics, which bars physicians from taking part in executions, and the World Medical Association's Declaration of Helsinki, which requires informed consent in research. My S10 calls them private professional rules. (standard meaning; site S10) ^g-ama-and-helsinki-codes

**America First** - A slogan for putting U.S. interests first in foreign and trade policy, used by the Trump administration. My site's version: "I stand for America First realism with selective Western imperialism," meaning the United States should prioritize its sovereignty, security, cultural cohesion, and manufacturing strength (S8). (site S8; standard meaning) ^g-america-first

**Amnesty** - Granting legal status to people living in the country unlawfully. I oppose it. (standard meaning) ^g-amnesty

**Anarcho-capitalism** - The view that the state should be abolished and every service, including law, courts, and defense, supplied by private markets. The report uses it for Hans-Hermann Hoppe; I want a constitutional republic instead. (standard meaning) ^g-anarcho-capitalism

**Anti-naturalism (sentientist)** - Here, the rejection of nature as a moral standard, not the metaethical term. In S1 I say nature may be "ethically indifferent or abominable," and in S7 that "Nature needs to be civilized." The label is the report's, not my site's. (standard meaning; site S1, S7) ^g-anti-naturalism-sentientist

**Anti-totalitarian parity** - The report's label for my treating the excluded ideologies on the same terms in public memory. I believe the state should officially condemn all four and remove public honors for communist figures, while praising Nazism or communism stays legal. (site S1, S2, S13 (report label)) ^g-anti-totalitarian-parity

**Antitrust** - Law against monopolies and anti-competitive conduct. My view: "big is not illegal." (standard meaning; site S5) ^g-antitrust

**Aquaculture and breeding mills** - Aquaculture is fish and shellfish farming; breeding mills are commercial operations that breed animals, such as dogs, in volume for sale. I believe both should end on day one. (standard meaning; site S9) ^g-aquaculture-and-breeding-mills

**Assimilationism** - The view that newcomers should adopt the host society's values and way of life. In my selection order, Western-values fit and assimilation come first and American labor needs second (S8). (standard meaning; site S2, S8) ^g-assimilationism

**Assisted death** - A competent adult ending their own life with medical help. I believe it should be allowed, with safeguards. (standard meaning; site S7) ^g-assisted-death

**Atkins v. Virginia (2002)** - Supreme Court case barring the execution of people with intellectual disability. (standard meaning) ^g-atkins-v-virginia-2002

**Autonomous vehicles rule** - My rule that a self-driving car's programming should never choose to sacrifice an identified rights-holder. (site S10) ^g-autonomous-vehicles-rule

**Autonomous weapons and space weapons** - Weapons that can select and strike targets without a human decision, and weapons based in space. I accept autonomous weapons only with human judgment kept, and defensive space weapons only. (site S8) ^g-autonomous-weapons-and-space-weapons

**Axioms (S1)** - The seven numbered principles at the base of my framework in S1, such as Axiom 5 (sentience set by revisable scientific consensus), Axiom 6 (the forfeiture threshold), and Axiom 7 (my system as "a coherent system I choose to endorse and want enforced"). (site S1) ^g-axioms-s1

## B

**Bailouts** - Government rescues of failing firms or banks with public money. I oppose them. (standard meaning) ^g-bailouts

**Bioconservatism** - Opposition to human enhancement and to reproductive technologies such as cloning, usually on grounds of human dignity or the value of the natural, as argued by Leon Kass. The report places me against this camp. (standard meaning) ^g-bioconservatism

**Birthright citizenship** - Automatic citizenship for anyone born on U.S. soil, under the 14th Amendment as now read. I believe it should end by amendment, going forward only. (standard meaning; site S8) ^g-birthright-citizenship

**BJS, BLS, CBO** - Federal statistics and budget agencies: the Bureau of Justice Statistics, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and the Congressional Budget Office. (standard meaning) ^g-bjs-bls-cbo

**Body cameras** - Cameras worn by police and other officers to record their encounters. I want them everywhere, including for ICE. (standard meaning) ^g-body-cameras

**Brain-computer interfaces** - Devices that connect the brain directly to a computer. I support them with opt-in neural data. (standard meaning; site S10) ^g-brain-computer-interfaces

**Building bar** - The bar on building, rebuilding, expanding, or relocating places that promote any of the four excluded ideologies, such as a Nazi gathering place or an Islamic center or school; new mosques are barred outright. "The bar falls on the place and the symbols built into it, never on a person's belief, speech, or home practice" (S3). (site S3) ^g-building-bar

**Bulk tracking, facial recognition, and social credit** - Mass collection of location or other data on the population, automated identification of faces, scoring citizens' behavior, and selling personal data. I believe each should be opt-in, with a high bar for bulk tracking. (site S3) ^g-bulk-tracking-facial-recognition-and-social-credit

**Burden-of-proof skepticism** - The rule that whoever makes a claim has to prove it, and that an unproven claim should not be believed. It grounds my agnostic atheism. (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-burden-of-proof-skepticism

## C

**Capital evidentiary standard** - See [[Glossary#Capital evidentiary standard|Capital evidentiary standard]] on the Glossary page. ^g-capital-evidentiary-standard

**Capital track** - Crimes I would treat as capital: punished by death (public) or the experimentation track, under the capital evidentiary standard. They include murder, violent rape, child molestation, kidnapping, trafficking, selling hard drugs, cruelty causing lasting injury, and killing a high-class animal. (site S1, S6) ^g-capital-track

**Capture of Maduro** - The January 3, 2026 U.S. military raid that captured Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro to face drug-trafficking charges. I grade it Aligned. (site S12) ^g-capture-of-maduro

**Carbon price** - A tax or cap-and-trade system that charges for carbon emissions. I oppose it. (standard meaning) ^g-carbon-price

**Carryover basis** - A rule that an heir takes over the original owner's purchase price for tax purposes, so gains built up before death are taxed when the heir sells. I would use it instead of an estate tax: "tax an heir's inherited gains when the assets are sold." (standard meaning; site S5) ^g-carryover-basis

**Cartels as terrorists** - My view that drug cartels, and governments that shelter them, are national-security threats to be defeated by force, including war on governments that refuse to cooperate. (site S8) ^g-cartels-as-terrorists

**CAT** - The UN Convention against Torture (1984), ratified by the U.S. in 1994. I believe the U.S. should denounce (withdraw from) it if needed, while keeping the bar on removing anyone to torture in statute. (standard meaning; site S8) ^g-cat

**Catastrophe threshold** - In standard threshold deontology, the point where the bad consequences of respecting a right become so large (a catastrophe) that the right may be overridden. My threshold is different: it is the agent's own violation of another's rights. (standard meaning) ^g-catastrophe-threshold

**CEO, IQ, ID, U.S., UK, D.C.** - CEO is chief executive officer; IQ is a standardized intelligence score; ID is identification; U.S. is the United States; UK is the United Kingdom; D.C. is Washington, D.C. (standard meaning) ^g-ceo-iq-id-us-uk-dc

**Cephalopods, decapods, bivalves** - Cephalopods are octopuses, squid, and cuttlefish; decapods are ten-legged crustaceans such as crabs, lobsters, and shrimp; bivalves are two-shelled animals such as clams, oysters, and mussels. My site places cephalopods and decapod crustaceans in the high sentience class and bivalves on the precautionary tier. (standard meaning; site Glossary (Sentience class)) ^g-cephalopods-decapods-bivalves

**Chapter 188 (Massachusetts)** - A 2026 Massachusetts abortion law that loosened later-term rules. My site cites it as an example of limits drifting past 12 weeks, which is why I believe the ban should run from conception. (site Glossary (Abortion ban); site S13) ^g-chapter-188-massachusetts

**Charter schools** - Publicly funded schools run independently of the local school district. My per-child funding can follow a child to them. (standard meaning) ^g-charter-schools

**Child credit and birth credit** - My proposed family credits: a child credit that doubles from the third child and a birth credit for third and later children, meant to raise births under lower immigration. (site S5) ^g-child-credit-and-birth-credit

**Christian fundamentalism** - A strict, literal form of Christianity that seeks to shape law and public life by religious teaching. My Start Here lists it as a Republican break; I believe religion should stay out of government. (standard meaning) ^g-christian-fundamentalism

**Civic disabilities** - My site's term for the two restrictions on excluded ideologies, the office bar and the building bar. My site says they "are not penalties": no one is fined, jailed, or silenced for belief. (site S1, S2) ^g-civic-disabilities

**Civil asset forfeiture** - A legal process in which the government seizes property it says is tied to crime through a civil case against the property itself, often without convicting or even charging the owner. Unlike rights forfeiture in my framework, I believe civil forfeiture without conviction should end. (standard meaning; site S3) ^g-civil-asset-forfeiture

**Civil libertarianism** - Strong protection of individual freedoms, such as privacy, speech, and a fair trial, against the state. "Procedural" stresses safeguards on how the state acts: I believe data, facial recognition, and social credit should be opt-in, and I want body cameras everywhere, a high bar for bulk tracking, and no qualified immunity. (standard meaning; site S3, S6) ^g-civil-libertarianism

**Civil service merit hiring** - Hiring government workers by qualification rather than politics. I believe policy roles should serve at the pleasure of elected officials, with fast removal for stated reasons. (site S5) ^g-civil-service-merit-hiring

**Civilizational nationalism** - My stance that a nation should protect and pass on a civilization defined by shared Western values, not by ethnicity: "a set of values, not an ethnicity." It drives my immigration selection, which puts values fit and assimilation first (S2, S8). (site S2) ^g-civilizational-nationalism

**Classical liberalism** - The tradition of limited government, individual rights, free markets, and the rule of law, from Locke and Adam Smith through Milton Friedman. (standard meaning) ^g-classical-liberalism

**Close-in-age exception** - A rule, sometimes called a "Romeo and Juliet" clause, that relationships between teenagers close in age are not treated as statutory rape. I believe the age of consent should be 18 with such an exception. (standard meaning; site S6, S7) ^g-close-in-age-exception

**Coercive conversion methods** - Forcible or abusive attempts to change a person's sexual orientation or gender identity. I believe they should be banned, while exploratory therapy (talking through a person's feelings without a set goal) should be allowed. (site S7) ^g-coercive-conversion-methods

**Coherentism** - The idea that a set of beliefs is justified by how well its parts fit together, rather than by outside foundations. "Coherentist prescriptivism" is an alternative label for my metaethics: in S1 I prescribe my system "because it is the most coherent and stably consistent framework available." (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-coherentism

**Coker v. Georgia (1977)** - Supreme Court case barring the death penalty for the rape of an adult woman where the victim is not killed. (standard meaning) ^g-coker-v-georgia-1977

**Colorblind meritocracy** - The view that law and institutions should not treat people differently by race and should reward individual merit. On my site, "Every offender should be judged as an individual, by the same law," and "race doesn't matter in itself; culture and assimilation do" (S6, S7). (standard meaning; site S6, S7) ^g-colorblind-meritocracy

**Companions-in-guilt argument** - A reply to moral anti-realism: if moral facts are too strange to exist, so are other facts we accept, such as reasons for belief, so the anti-realist argument proves too much. S1 answers this reply in full. (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-companions-in-guilt-argument

**Compelled hard labor** - My rule that every sentence would include supervised hard labor, with no idle incarceration. (site Glossary (Mark); site S6) ^g-compelled-hard-labor

**Competence qualification** - The second half of my qualified franchise: passing the voter license test on issues, civics, and laws. (site S3) ^g-competence-qualification

**Competing currencies** - Letting private or alternative currencies circulate alongside the dollar. (standard meaning) ^g-competing-currencies

**Constitutionalism** - Government limited by a written constitution and the rule of law, so that officials can't act beyond the powers it grants. (standard meaning) ^g-constitutionalism

**Constructivism (Humean)** - In metaethics, the view that moral norms are built from the attitudes and choices of agents rather than discovered as outside facts. "Humean" means rooted in human sentiment and choice, after David Hume. S1 calls my view "constructivist in the Humean sense." (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-constructivism-humean

**Corporate personhood (agent-liability skepticism)** - Corporate personhood is the legal treatment of a corporation as a person that can own property, sue, and be sued. The report's label for my view: corporations are tools, not moral agents, so I believe the CEO should be liable by default, fines should reach up to 4% of global revenue, and the corporate veil should be pierced for deliberate violations. (standard meaning; site S1, S6) ^g-corporate-personhood-agent-liability-skepticism

**Corporate veil** - The legal separation that normally protects owners and managers from a company's debts and liabilities. "Piercing the veil" means a court holds them personally liable anyway, which I believe should happen for deliberate violations. (standard meaning; site S6) ^g-corporate-veil

**Cosmopolitan liberalism** - Liberalism that gives equal moral weight to every person regardless of nationality, usually favoring open borders and global institutions. Jason Brennan pairs it with epistocracy; I pair epistocracy with civilizational nationalism. (standard meaning) ^g-cosmopolitan-liberalism

**Cultural traditionalism** - A preference for traditional family and social forms. Mine is "preference, not mandate": I believe traditional roles are best but should never be mandated. (standard meaning; site S7) ^g-cultural-traditionalism

## D

**Day fines (income-scaled fines)** - See [[Glossary#Income-scaled fine|Income-scaled fine]] on the Glossary page. ^g-day-fines-income-scaled-fines

**Day-one bans** - My position that animal agriculture, fishing, aquaculture, breeding mills, and animal testing should be "banned outright and instantly," with "no lag at all" and no gradual wind-down (S1). (site S1, S9) ^g-day-one-bans

**Death row** - The years-long wait between a death sentence and execution. I want 30-day capital trials and same-day execution, with no death row. (standard meaning; site S6) ^g-death-row

**Debt brake** - See [[Glossary#Debt brake|Debt brake]] on the Glossary page. ^g-debt-brake

**Deceptive candidacies** - Candidates entered mainly to mislead voters or split the vote. I believe the rules for handling them should be fixed before the election (S3 Ballot Access and Deceptive Candidacies). (standard meaning; site S3) ^g-deceptive-candidacies

**Deepfakes** - AI-generated video, audio, or images that realistically show someone saying or doing something they didn't. I treat deepfakes as speech (S10, S13). (standard meaning; site S10) ^g-deepfakes

**Default enrollment (opt-out defaults)** - Signing people up automatically while letting them leave freely. I would use it only where a person protects themselves, as with private disability insurance, retirement accounts, and index accounts, citing Madrian and Shea's finding that 401(k) participation rose from 37% to 86% under automatic enrollment. (site S5, S10) ^g-default-enrollment-opt-out-defaults

**Defensive accelerationism** - A stance, often called "d/acc," that favors fast technological progress while giving priority to defensive technologies against catastrophic risk. The report applies it to me as an inference: pro-AI, with AI regulated as an existential risk and a defensive AGI prepared (S10). (standard meaning (report inference)) ^g-defensive-accelerationism

**DEI** - Diversity, equity, and inclusion: programs that give weight to group identity in hiring, admissions, and training. I believe in no quotas or DEI. (standard meaning; site S7) ^g-dei

**Deontology** - Ethics built on duties and rights that may not be violated, rather than on producing the best outcomes. Its main rival is utilitarianism. (standard meaning) ^g-deontology

**Desert** - See [[Glossary#Desert|Desert]] on the Glossary page. ^g-desert

**Deterrence** - Justifying punishment by its power to discourage future crime. I do not ground punishment in deterrence; I ground it in desert, incapacitation, and forfeiture. (standard meaning; site S6) ^g-deterrence

**Directional (transactional) independent** - A voter with no party identity who backs whichever side moves policy most in their direction. My homepage calls my support "a cost analysis," and S11 says "No modern political party aligns with my entire system." (site homepage, S11 (report label)) ^g-directional-transactional-independent

**Disparate impact** - The legal rule, from Griggs v. Duke Power Co. (1971) and Title VII, that a neutral practice causing a disparate impact by race, sex, or another protected trait is unlawful unless the employer shows it is job-related and a business necessity. I believe disparate-impact liability should be repealed. (site S7) ^g-disparate-impact

**Displacement trigger (BLS trigger)** - My rule for AI job loss: after four straight quarters with the BLS labor share at least 3 points below its ten-year average and the prime-age employment-to-population ratio at least 2 points below its level three years earlier, the tax on capital income would rise from 15% to the 20% flat rate, then by a surcharge of up to 5 points after eight quarters, never above 25% including the transition surcharge and the Sanctuary Levy. Each step reverses when the measures recover for four quarters. (site S10) ^g-displacement-trigger-bls-trigger

**Double jeopardy** - The Fifth Amendment rule that a person can't be tried twice for the same offense after an acquittal or conviction. My retrial-after-nullification rule departs from it, which I acknowledge (S3). (standard meaning; site S3) ^g-double-jeopardy

**DSA** - Democratic Socialists of America, a left-wing political organization. (standard meaning) ^g-dsa

## E

**Earnings-only top-up** - See [[Glossary#Earnings-only top-up|Earnings-only top-up]] on the Glossary page. ^g-earnings-only-top-up

**East Potomac golf closure** - The Park Service's 2026 move to close the public golf course at East Potomac Park in Washington for redevelopment, now paused by a court order. I grade it Directionally opposed. (site S12) ^g-east-potomac-golf-closure

**Eco-modernism** - Environmentalism that relies on technology, growth, and dense energy sources like nuclear power to shrink human impact, instead of cutting consumption. Mine is "reliability first, no mandates, nuclear yes." (standard meaning; site S9) ^g-eco-modernism

**Economic nationalism** - Using tariffs, mandates, and industrial policy to build up domestic industry. I prefer tax and regulatory relief to tariffs and mandates. (standard meaning) ^g-economic-nationalism

**Eighth Amendment** - The part of the U.S. Constitution that bans "cruel and unusual punishments" and excessive bail and fines. I believe it should be amended or abolished. (standard meaning; site S6) ^g-eighth-amendment

**EITC and CTC** - The Earned Income Tax Credit (a refundable credit for low- and moderate-income workers) and the Child Tax Credit (a per-child credit). I believe the earnings-only top-up should replace both. (standard meaning; site S5) ^g-eitc-and-ctc

**El Salvador-style sweeps** - Mass arrests of gang members of the kind Nayib Bukele's government carried out in El Salvador under a state of exception from 2022. I believe they should be allowed only inside a declared, time-limited emergency. (site S3, S6, S13) ^g-el-salvador-style-sweeps

**Electoral College** - The system where states' electors, not the national popular vote, choose the President. I would keep it, with electors chosen by ranked choice. (standard meaning; site S3) ^g-electoral-college

**Emergency on-scene sentencing** - My position that inside a declared, time-limited emergency, police or military could sentence on the spot. The capital standard, body cameras, and later review would still apply, and an officer whose sentence is not supported would be charged with murder. (site S3, S6) ^g-emergency-on-scene-sentencing

**Emergency powers and sunset clauses** - Emergency powers are special powers a government claims in a crisis; a sunset clause makes a law or power expire on a set date unless renewed. I believe emergencies should be necessary, narrow, temporary, and expire automatically unless the legislature renews them. (standard meaning; site S3, S12) ^g-emergency-powers-and-sunset-clauses

**Empiricism (fallibilist)** - Empiricism holds that knowledge comes from experience and observation. Fallibilism adds that any belief, however well supported, could be wrong and stays open to revision, as with my rule that sentience classes be set by scientific consensus and be revisable (Axiom 5). (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-empiricism-fallibilist

**Energy emergency and D.C. Guard deployment** - The national energy emergency declared by Executive Order 14156 in January 2025 and renewed by the President, and the National Guard deployment in Washington, D.C. I fault both for open-ended renewals. (site S12) ^g-energy-emergency-and-dc-guard-deployment

**Epistemology** - The study of knowledge: what counts as evidence and when a belief is justified. (standard meaning) ^g-epistemology

**Epistocracy** - "Epistocracy means rule by the knowledgeable." The term is Jason Brennan's. In my system the vote would go to citizens who meet a stake qualification and a competence qualification. (site Glossary; site S3) ^g-epistocracy

**Epistocratic republic** - See [[Glossary#Epistocratic republic|Epistocratic republic]] on the Glossary page. ^g-epistocratic-republic

**ESA "harm" rule (Endangered Species Act)** - The Endangered Species Act. Its regulatory definition of "harm," which since 1981 covered habitat damage that actually kills or injures listed wildlife, was rescinded effective September 14, 2026. I grade that Opposed. (site S12) ^g-esa-harm-rule-endangered-species-act

**ESA (education savings account)** - An education savings account: state money put into an account parents can spend on private school and other approved education costs. Not the same as the Endangered Species Act. I oppose them. (standard meaning; site S5) ^g-esa-education-savings-account

**Estate, wealth, VAT, and sales taxes** - An estate tax is levied on what a person leaves at death; a wealth tax on total net worth each year; a VAT (value-added tax) and a sales tax on purchases. I believe none should exist at any level of government. (standard meaning; site S5) ^g-estate-wealth-vat-and-sales-taxes

**Ethnonationalism** - Nationalism that defines the nation by shared ancestry or ethnicity. My test is about values and open to any ancestry. (standard meaning) ^g-ethnonationalism

**Eudaimonism (Stoic)** - Ethics aimed at eudaimonia, a flourishing life lived through virtue. The Stoic version ties flourishing to virtue, composure, and accepting what one can't control. I use it for personal character only, not for politics. (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-eudaimonism-stoic

**Evidentialism** - The rule that belief should match the evidence. My standard in S1: "Proof here means testable, repeatable evidence or reasoning that others can check." (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-evidentialism

**Evolutionary debunking** - The argument (Sharon Street, Richard Joyce) that evolution shaped our moral beliefs for survival rather than truth, which undercuts the claim that they track objective moral facts. It is one of my three arguments for anti-realism in S1. (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-evolutionary-debunking

**Ex post facto** - Punishing an act that was legal when it was done. I believe no one should be punished for animal killings before the ban. (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-ex-post-facto

**Excluded ideologies** - The four ideologies my proposed constitutional amendment names: Nazism, communism, socialism, and Islam, held on the same terms. I believe their adherents should be barred from sworn authority roles (the office bar) and that places promoting them should be capped (the building bar). No one would be fined, jailed, removed, or silenced for holding or advocating any view. (site S1; site Glossary (Office bar)) ^g-excluded-ideologies

**Executive discretion over federal property** - My view that the President may decide on monuments and improvements on property the executive already controls, preferably privately or opt-in funded, but that this discretion "is not a power to take new land, spend money Congress has not appropriated, or go beyond the laws that authorize the executive." (site S5, S12) ^g-executive-discretion-over-federal-property

**Experimentation track** - A sentence for offenders who have forfeited the right to life (the capital tier or three marks): instead of execution, they would be used as research subjects in place of animals. I believe the victim should choose between execution and experimentation; where the victim is dead or there is no single victim, the judge should choose and may not refuse. Harm outside the sentence, such as guard abuse unrelated to a study, should still be punished. (site S6, S10) ^g-experimentation-track

**Expressivism** - The view that moral statements express attitudes such as approval or disapproval rather than describe facts. S1 says my view has "an expressive element." (standard meaning; site S1) ^g-expressivism

## F

**FDA consent rules** - Food and Drug Administration rules requiring informed consent for clinical trials of drugs and devices. (standard meaning) ^g-fda-consent-rules

**Fed (Federal Reserve)** - The U.S. central bank. I believe in a single price-stability rule, no bailouts, competing currencies, no gold standard, and governors removable only for cause. (standard meaning; site S5) ^g-fed-federal-reserve

**Federalism** - Dividing power between the national government and the states. Mine puts defense, borders, and immigration at the federal level and schools at the local level. (standard meaning) ^g-federalism
