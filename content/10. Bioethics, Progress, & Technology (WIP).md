Core Principle: Strong support for technological advancement and transhumanism, constrained only by sentient rights. Free-market and private innovation preferred; government role limited to regulation against existential threats, rights violations, and national security. Opt-in privacy and data rules; animal testing redirected to forfeited rights holders.

---


AI & Automation

- Regulate AI development as part of government’s duty to protect citizens.

- Treat existential risk seriously; prepare defensive AGI against dangerous AGI.

- Open to UBI as response to large-scale automation-driven job displacement.

- Pro-AI overall.

Genetic Engineering & CRISPR

- Support editing out negative traits.

- Designer babies acceptable if post-sentience rights are respected.

- Animal genetic engineering only when it advances (rather than violates) their sentient rights.

Transhumanism

- Fully supportive of enhancement technologies (brain-computer interfaces, life extension, cyborgs, etc.).

- Ethical limits apply only when the technology or its development violates sentient rights.

Abortion & Fetal Rights (Tech Impact)

- Advancing technologies (artificial wombs, earlier viability, better sentience detection) should only trigger policy changes if they create or clarify sentient-rights violations.

- Core sentience + responsibility framework remains the standard.

Animal Rights & Sentience Tech

- Lab-grown meat is the best path to a sentientist world and drastic reduction of animal agriculture.

- True AI-simulated sentience would receive full sentient rights protections.

- Animal testing completely banned.

- Cosmetic, pharmaceutical, and technological experimentation redirected to violent criminal offenders sentenced to death (their rights forfeited by egregious violations).

Surveillance & Digital Tech

- Data privacy fully respected; all data trading opt-in only.

- Government regulates and punishes violations.

- Public-space data has no privacy expectation and may be used for public protection and public interest.

- Private companies accessing or trading such data remain regulated.

Space & Exploration

- Human spaceflight fully privatized.

- Government space efforts limited to militarized programs and technology competition with foreign powers.

- Private companies handle civilian R&D; government may contract them for military/security needs.

Intellectual Property & Innovation

- Open-source generally preferable but not mandated.

- Intellectual property protections available for those who choose them (concept remains relatively loose).

- Government R&D primarily via contracts with private companies.

AI & Automation:  
  
Regulation of AI development? Universal basic income as response to job displacement? Alignment and existential risk concerns?  
  
AI should be monitored and regulated as following the governments role in protecting the best interest of its people. If it is observed that AI is becoming an existential threat, and it likely is, we need to have governmental focus on regulating its development, and preparing to defend against it. UBI is on the table as a response to heavy automation. I am Pro-AI and we will likely need a "defensive AGI" to combat any kind of dangerous AGI that emerges.  
  
  
Genetic Engineering & CRISPR:  
  
Human germline editing? Designer babies? Agricultural/animal applications?  
  
If we have the ability to genetically edit and modify against negative traits it should be done. I have no issue with designer babies if sentient rights are not violated post sentience. Any genetic engineering with animals must be done to benefit their sentient rights protections, not violate them.  
  
  
Transhumanism:  
  
Enhancement technologies (brain-computer interfaces, life extension, cyborgs)? Ethical limits?  
  
I fully support transhuman technology. The only ethical limits imposed are if the technology or its R&D violate sentient rights.  
  
  
Abortion & Fetal Rights:  
  
How should advancing tech (artificial wombs, earlier viability, sentience detection) affect policy?  
  
Policy should only affect advancing tech if sentient rights are violated.  
  
  
Animal Rights & Sentience Tech:  
  
How does your herbivore/sanctuary framework interact with lab-grown meat, animal testing, or AI-simulated sentience?  
  
Lab-grown meat is humanity's best hope in creating a sentientist world and vastly diminishing animal agriculture. AI sentience can be allowed. If artificial systems give way to true sentience, then said sentience would have full protections of sentient rights like biological beings. Animal testing should be completely banned - cosmetic, pharmaceutical, and technological experimentation should be performed on violent criminal offenders that are sentenced to death - their rights have been forfeited due to their egregious violations.  
  
  
Surveillance & Digital Tech:  
  
Facial recognition, social scoring, data privacy in the tech age?  
  
All data privacy should be respected. All data trading should be opt-in only. Government to regulate this and punish any transgressions. Data acquired in public spaces should have no expectation of privacy protection. It should only be used for public protections and the best interest of the people. There should be government regulation regarding any private company trading or accessing this encrypted data.  
  
  
Space & Exploration:  
  
Prioritize human spaceflight, colonization, or private enterprise?  
  
Human spaceflight should be completely privatized. The only governmental space program that should be funded should be militarized and weighing and advancing technology to compete with any foreign power. If people of the country want to fly in space, private companies should do R&D and create the technology. The government can purchase or contract private companies for their military research or security programs in this industry.  
  
  
Intellectual Property & Innovation:  
  
Strong patents or open-source preference? Role of government in R&D?  
  
Open-source is generally preferable but it should not be mandated. If someone wants to mark intellectual property, it should be protected, though intellectual property is relatively loose and undefined as a concept. Government R&D can contract private companies.
