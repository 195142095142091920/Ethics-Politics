## Core Principles
A short tour of my positions. Each section below is introduced in my own words, drawn from that section's opening principles, with a link to read more. Key terms are defined in the [[Glossary]], and every topic's one-line position is collected in [[Where I Stand]].

## [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework]]
- **Sentientist:** I believe moral status and full negative rights belong to beings capable of first-order subjective experience. A being on a clear developmental trajectory toward sentience (e.g. a healthy fetus) gets significant moral weight in my view, not the full negative-rights stack. On elective abortion, I would ban it from 12 weeks by constitutional amendment as a rights statute; any limit earlier than 12 weeks I label prudential, based on drift evidence, with a sunset ([[Glossary#Abortion rights floor (12 weeks)|Abortion rights floor (12 weeks)]]).
- **Negative-rights focused:** I treat rights as side-constraints against interference and harm. I generally reject positive rights that compel others to provide goods or services. I accept minimal tax-funded basic services (education, public safety, infrastructure) as a narrow practical exception.
- **Threshold deontologist:** I hold that core rules against violating sentient rights normally stand. They may be overridden only after a clear threshold of rights violation is crossed, at which point the violator forfeits the corresponding protections. I believe fetal moral weight scales with proximity to sentience and rests primarily on the responsibility created by voluntary procreation. Duties to sustain another being arise only from voluntary responsibility; where no voluntary act created the dependency, as in rape, I hold that withdrawing support differs from killing.

## [[2. Political and Social Character]]
I give priority to cultural cohesion, high-trust social conditions, national sovereignty, and strong borders. I believe the state's primary duties are to its own citizens and to the long-term continuity of the political community. Immigration should be selective and oriented toward compatibility with the shared Western value structure, judged at the country level and through values vetting of every immigrant, and I treat cultural assimilation as paramount. The border must be secured, with lethal force used when necessary. To me "Western" names a value structure, not an ethnicity, so compatibility is open to anyone who holds those values.

---

## [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties]]
I support strong individual liberties and property rights, with government limited to protecting sentient rights, public safety, and order. I want opt-in mechanisms where possible and harsh accountability for criminals and officials. I am skeptical of overreach but support effective enforcement. I believe voting should require ID, a knowledge exam, and eligibility that excludes [[Glossary#Felony and misdemeanor|felons]] and restricts the franchise to net taxpayers (conflict of interest).

---

## [[4. Religion & Secularism]]
I support strict separation of religion and government in the United States. Religions that do not violate sentient rights should be tolerated; those that do should be restricted in their harmful aspects. **Office bar:** I would bar adherents of the four excluded ideologies (Nazism, communism, socialism, Islam) from authority roles - elected or appointed office; law enforcement, military, and intelligence; judges and prosecutors; immigration and other adjudicators; K-12 public curriculum authority - under the excluded-ideologies amendment ([[4. Religion & Secularism#^office-bar|Oath of Office]]; [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^excluded-ideologies|Excluded ideologies]]). Ordinary non-authority public jobs stay open. I protect private conscience and home practice, but I want no new mosques, and public institutions ought to remain secular.

---

## [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance]]
I believe in limited government, individual responsibility, low taxation, and maximum cultural and economic freedom, with only minimal safety nets. The state should prioritize free markets, personal choice, and fiscal restraint. I accept opt-in social programs and basic public services; I reject mandatory redistribution beyond the narrow exceptions named in Welfare and Entitlements. Minimal necessary taxes should fund core services: K-12 education, police, fire, emergency services, infrastructure upkeep, and the bare-minimum safety floor defined in Welfare and Entitlements. I would have each state fund K-12 from its general revenue at about \$10,000 per child, as set out in [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Education|Education]].

---

## [[6. Criminal Justice & Security]]
I put retribution, incapacitation, and the protection of victims' sentient rights ahead of rehabilitation. I believe those who murder or commit violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, or the sale or distribution of hard drugs, or who repeatedly violate the rights of others, forfeit the protections they denied their victims. Punishment ought to be swift, certain, and public, and capital punishment should apply only where guilt is established beyond any real doubt. I support heavy enforcement, with military-style action against cartels and high-crime threats.

---

## [[7. Cultural & Social Issues]]
I prefer the traditional nuclear family, and I support free speech absolutism (with narrow exceptions), meritocracy, cultural compatibility for cohesion, and personal responsibility. I strongly oppose identity politics and progressive social engineering, and I am skeptical of mandates.

---

## [[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations]]
I hold to America First realism with selective Western imperialism. I believe the United States should prioritize its sovereignty, security, cultural cohesion, and manufacturing strength. It ought to engage forcefully against direct threats (especially Iran and extremist Islam) while avoiding open-ended nation-building, and join alliances and institutions only when they clearly serve American interests.

---

## [[9. Energy & Environment]]
I want a practical, gradual shift driven by markets and private innovation, within sentient-rights limits: no energy mandates, no early forced shutdowns, and no taxpayer-funded subsidies without opt-in. I put human (and herbivore) welfare ahead of apocalyptic framing, and I believe nature should be conserved where it does not conflict with rights or development.

---

## [[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology]]
I strongly support technological advancement and transhumanism, constrained only by sentient rights. I prefer free-market and private innovation, and I believe government's role should be limited to regulation against existential threats, rights violations, and national security. I want opt-in privacy and data rules, and animal testing redirected to forfeited rights holders.
