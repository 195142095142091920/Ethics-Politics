## [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework]]
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Applications elsewhere|Applications elsewhere]]:** Animal agriculture and aquaculture are banned outright and instantly; any gradual change in the real world is an aside, like taxes or education.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Conflicts Between Rights-Holders|Conflicts Between Rights-Holders]]:** Full moral agency needs sentience plus intentional action and foresight, and it scales with sophistication.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Stoic Eudaimonism|Stoic Eudaimonism]]:** Stoic eudaimonism is the personal operating system for character: focus on what is within one's control, practice wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance, and keep composure while acting decisively.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Limits of Politics|Limits of Politics]]:** The state exists to protect negative rights and the security of the community that sustains them, not to equalize outcomes.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Corporations as Moral Entities|Corporations as Moral Entities]]:** Corporations are tools, not moral agents.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Duties to Future Generations|Duties to Future Generations]]:** Present agents may not impose extreme, unjustified risk of death or rights destruction on future sentient beings.

- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Economic Orientation|Economic Orientation]]:** Free markets, a flat tax with a large zero bracket, opt-in welfare, no business subsidies without opt-in, regulation that must justify itself, and balanced budgets with defense first.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Cultural Stance|Cultural Stance]]:** Traditional family life is the preferred default but not a legal mandate, merit replaces identity politics, speech is near-absolute, and the government condemns Islamism and communism without punishing belief.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Order and Security|Order and Security]]:** Crime is met hard: a mandatory death penalty for murder (including attempted murder), violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and human trafficking, forfeiture for repeat offenders, and decisive action against cartels, all under due process and police oversight, with no penalty for belief.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#The Future and Technology|The Future and Technology]]:** Technology is embraced within sentient-rights limits: AI and genetics are supported, data is opt-in, animal testing is banned, and sentient AI has rights and the same forfeiture rules as everyone else.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Western Values and Compatibility|Western Values and Compatibility]]:** "Western" means a set of values, not an ethnicity or a place: rights, liberty, religious freedom with church-state separation, rule of law, civic responsibility, and opportunity.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Relation to Other Political Traditions|Relation to Other Political Traditions]]:** I share the liberal and libertarian commitment to rights, property, and markets, but I accept far more force against criminals and at the border.

In practice, every state that tried to build it abolished private property by force, banned rival parties, and ran forced-labor camps, and several caused famines and mass killings that cost millions of lives. The framework condemns communism without making belief in it a crime.

It murdered six million Jews and millions of others, including disabled people, Roma, Poles, and Soviet prisoners of war. It is the clearest case of what the framework rejects: rights assigned by group identity instead of held by every sentient individual.

## [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties]]
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Individual Liberty vs State Power|Individual Liberty vs State Power]]:** The government should leave people alone unless they are actively harming others or about to do so.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]:** Emergency powers must be necessary, narrow, and temporary.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Government Structure and Checks|Government Structure and Checks]]:** Government exists to protect rights and must justify every expansion.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Term Limits|Term Limits]]:** No term limits on any elected office.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Federalism|Federalism]]:** Defense, borders, and immigration are federal; schools are local with a state funding floor.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Property Rights|Property Rights]]:** Once ownership is properly established, it is meant to last.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Self-Defense and Defense of Others|Self-Defense and Defense of Others]]:** People may use force to protect themselves or others anywhere, with no duty to retreat.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Freedom of Association|Freedom of Association]]:** People, clubs, and religious groups are free to choose whom they associate with.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Privacy Rights|Privacy Rights]]:** Privacy means control over access to one’s body, home, private spaces, and personal information.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]:** Everyone answers for what they do, and people with more power answer to a higher standard.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Civil Asset Forfeiture|Civil Asset Forfeiture]]:** The state does not take property from people who have not been convicted, except assets of foreign cartels formally designated as war enemies or Foreign Terrorist Organizations, which are seized as enemy property on the combatant track, and true contraband, which may be seized and destroyed or returned to owners.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Civil Service Protections|Civil Service Protections]]:** Hiring is by merit.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Pretrial Detention and Bail|Pretrial Detention and Bail]]:** Defendants are released before trial unless a judge finds they are dangerous or likely to flee.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Role of Juries|Role of Juries]]:** Conviction requires a unanimous jury of at least twelve, in civil and criminal cases alike.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Police and Law Enforcement|Police and Law Enforcement]]:** Police get more money, more officers, and more respect, and officers who break the law are punished more than ordinary citizens.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Voting and Elections|Voting and Elections]]:** Voters must show ID, mail-in ballots are fully authenticated and encrypted, and felons cannot vote.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Election Administration|Election Administration]]:** Elections require ID, authenticated mail ballots, and clean voter rolls, with money limited to individual donations.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Ranked-Choice Voting|Ranked-Choice Voting]]:** Ranked-choice voting is each state's choice, never a federal mandate or ban.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Direct Democracy and Citizen Initiatives|Direct Democracy and Citizen Initiatives]]:** States and localities may offer citizen initiatives and referendums. A constitutional amendment creates a national initiative for federal statutes and amendments, reaching the ballot with signatures from 1% (statute) or 2% (amendment) of qualified voters within 18 months.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Surveillance State|Surveillance State]]:** Facial recognition and social credit are opt-in only.

## [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance]]
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Economic System|Economic System]]:** **Markets work better than the alternatives for making people richer over time.** Countries and periods that protected private property and let prices and competition guide most decisions produced higher living standards, less extreme poverty, and more innovation than systems that put the state in charge of most production and prices.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Socialism|Socialism]]:** Socialism in its core sense, social or state ownership of the means of production and planning in place of markets, is incompatible with the framework because it overrides property and voluntary exchange. Social democracy is different: the Nordic countries are market economies with large welfare states. The framework shares the goal of a floor for basic needs but funds only a minimal one and leaves the rest to markets and voluntary giving.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Labor and Unions|Labor and Unions]]:** Unions are voluntary and no one has to join or pay one to work.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Antitrust|Antitrust]]:** Big is not illegal.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Taxation]]:** The main federal tax is simple: most people below a clear income line pay nothing, and those with low earnings receive a top-up on what they earn.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Welfare and Entitlements|Welfare and Entitlements]]:** People already on benefits who followed the rules keep their entitlement through a phase-out period; the funding behind it may change.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Child Support|Child Support]]:** Both genetic parents who consented to the sex owe support.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Social Security and Medicare Transition|Social Security and Medicare Transition]]:** Social Security and Medicare become voluntary: nobody is forced to pay in.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Housing, Zoning, and Homelessness|Housing, Zoning, and Homelessness]]:** Housing is left mainly to property owners and the market.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare|Healthcare]]:** People choose between a publicly supported plan and private insurance.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval|Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval]]:** The public health option is voluntary, and taxpayers fund it only by choice.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Private Education Oversight|Private Education Oversight]]:** Private and religious schools have the full right to exist and are privately funded.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Trade and Alternative Pathways|Trade and Alternative Pathways]]:** Basic K-12 education is publicly funded, as a fixed per-pupil amount (weighted for disability, with pre-K left out).
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Infrastructure and Regulation|Infrastructure and Regulation]]:** Taxes pay to keep core infrastructure safe and working.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Fiscal Policy|Fiscal Policy]]:** The budget should balance, deficits should be kept to a minimum, and the national debt should be paid down and eventually eliminated.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Monetary Policy|Monetary Policy]]:** Balanced budgets keep money stable.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public-Sector Size and Civil Service|Public-Sector Size and Civil Service]]:** Government is sized to its essential functions, with defense first.

## [[6. Criminal Justice & Security]]
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]:** Deliberate murder is punished by death, and so are violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and human trafficking proven to the same strict standard.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Lesser Homicide|Lesser Homicide]]:** A self-defense killing that went too far but was close to justified is a lesser homicide with a lighter sentence set by the judge.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Human Trafficking|Human Trafficking]]:** Human trafficking of adults or children, and kidnapping, are egregious rights violations and are capital.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Evidentiary Standard|Evidentiary Standard]]:** Nobody is executed unless the proof is overwhelming and backed by objective evidence such as video and physical evidence, not just testimony.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Wrongful Convictions and Compensation|Wrongful Convictions and Compensation]]:** Convicting an innocent person is treated as the state violating their rights.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row|Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row]]:** Capital trials take 30 days at most, and the sentence is carried out the day it is handed down.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life|Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life]]:** Repeat offenders who keep committing crimes after being punished lose their right to life once they reach three marks, provided they are still active. At least one earlier mark must fall within the ten years before the new crime.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Juvenile Offenders|Juvenile Offenders]]:** There is no fixed age of criminal responsibility; how severely a minor is punished depends on how serious the crime was.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Age of Consent|Age of Consent]]:** The age of consent is 18, except under the close-in-age exception below, and a minor cannot consent to sex with an adult.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Insanity Defense|Insanity Defense]]:** Mental illness does not undo a rights violation: a mentally handicapped person who kills still commits murder.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Civil Commitment|Civil Commitment]]:** No one is confined for being homeless or mentally ill.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Statutes of Limitations|Statutes of Limitations]]:** Murder, violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and human trafficking can be prosecuted no matter how much time has passed, as long as the strict evidence standard is met.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor|Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor]]:** No one is imprisoned for life with nothing to do.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice|Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice]]:** The justice system is first about protecting victims and the public and giving offenders what they deserve; reconciling offenders with victims or reintegrating them comes second and never changes the sentence.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Public Executions|Public Executions]]:** Executions are public or openly recorded, so citizens can see what the state does in their name.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment|Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment]]:** Studies cannot say whether the death penalty deters, partly because the current system is so slow and uncertain.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police Power and Accountability|Police Power and Accountability]]:** Police get more money, more officers, and more respect, but officers who break the law are punished more harshly than ordinary people.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Drug Policy|Drug Policy]]:** Using drugs privately is not a crime, but using them in public is banned.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Border Security and Immigration Enforcement|Border Security and Immigration Enforcement]]:** Illegal immigrants are removed by force, the border is heavily fortified, and lethal force is allowed when necessary.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Terrorism and Domestic Extremism]]:** Domestic terrorism is treated as a serious threat.
- **My view on political violence:** The 2020 riots were left-wing political violence that terrorism counts leave out, and I condemn both sides' violence equally.
- **My view on race and crime:** I acknowledge a great disproportion in violence and crime between Black Americans and others. I believe this is likely due to a lack of family structure and a generally corrosive culture that glorifies the wrong aspects of what creates a lasting society: disdain for education, aspiration for gangs, disrespect for peers, seeing crime as virtuous, and a permanent victim mentality with no accountability.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Gun Crime and Violence]]:** Gang and high-crime areas get heavy, hot-spot policing and fast, harsh crackdowns, and high-rate violent offenders are locked up, with full due process.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Domestic Violence|Domestic Violence]]:** Extreme violence, intentional neglect, beating without drawing blood, and withholding food, water, or shelter as punishment against a spouse or family member are capital if they cause lasting injury. Otherwise they are egregious: three marks at once: execution, experimentation, or life with labor.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Victims' Rights|Victims' Rights]]:** Offenders lose their rights when they violate others'.
