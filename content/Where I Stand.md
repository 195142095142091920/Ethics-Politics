## [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework]]
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Moral Psychology|Moral Psychology]]:** Full moral agency needs sentience plus intentional action and foresight, and that it scales with sophistication.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Animal Rights and Sentientism|Animal Rights and Sentientism]]:** Animal agriculture and aquaculture should be banned outright and shut down immediately, with no wind-down and sanctuary for every animal still alive ([[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Sanctuary Plan]]).
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Stoic Eudaimonism|Stoic Eudaimonism]]:** I use Stoic eudaimonism as my personal operating system for character: focus on what is within my control, practice wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance, and keep composure while acting decisively.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Limits of Politics|Limits of Politics]]:** The state exists to protect negative rights and the security of the community that sustains them, not to equalize outcomes.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Corporations as Moral Entities|Corporations as Moral Entities]]:** I see corporations as tools, not moral agents.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Duties to Future Generations|Duties to Future Generations]]:** Present agents may not impose extreme, unjustified risk of death or rights destruction on future sentient beings.

## [[2. Political and Social Character]]
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Civilizational Nationalism|Civilizational Nationalism]]:** The country should put its own citizens first, keep strong borders, and admit immigrants who share Western values and assimilate.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Western Values and Compatibility|Western Values and Compatibility]]:** To me, "Western" means a set of values, not an ethnicity or a place: rights, liberty, religious freedom with church-state separation, rule of law, civic responsibility, and opportunity. Being Muslim creates a presumption of failing the values test, which an individual can overcome only with verified evidence of conduct and record.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Economic Orientation|Economic Orientation]]:** I support free markets, a flat tax with a large zero bracket, opt-in welfare, no business subsidies without opt-in, regulation that must justify itself, and balanced budgets with defense first. I accept two time-limited exceptions to the single flat rate: a 2-point transition surcharge through 2041 that goes to the debt ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#^surcharge|Taxation]]), and a flat Sanctuary Levy on income above \$75,000 single or \$150,000 joint, never above 3%, that pays for sanctuary care and ends when the sanctuary population falls below 1% of its starting animal units and its bonds are repaid ([[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Sanctuary Plan]]).
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Cultural Stance|Cultural Stance]]:** I prefer traditional family life as the default but not as a legal mandate. Merit should replace identity politics, speech should be near-absolute, and the government should condemn Nazism, communism, socialism, and Islam without punishing belief.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Order and Security|Order and Security]]:** Crime should be met hard: a mandatory death penalty for murder (including attempted murder), violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, and selling or distributing hard drugs, forfeiture for repeat offenders, and decisive action against cartels, under due process and police oversight outside emergencies, with no penalty for belief; the two civic disabilities for [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^excluded-ideologies|excluded ideologies]] are not penalties.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#The Future and Technology|The Future and Technology]]:** I embrace technology within sentient-rights limits: I support AI and genetics, data should be opt-in, all animal testing should end immediately, and sentient AI should have rights and the same forfeiture rules as everyone else.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Relation to Other Political Traditions|Relation to Other Political Traditions]]:** I share the liberal and libertarian commitment to rights, property, and markets, but I accept far more force against criminals and at the border.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Communism in Practice|Communism in Practice]]:** Communist theory promises a classless society in which "the free development of each is the condition for the free development of all." In practice, every state that tried to build it abolished private property by force, banned rival parties, and ran forced-labor camps, and several caused famines and mass killings that cost millions of lives. Communism is condemned at the same severity as Nazism, socialism, and Islam, and the state treats its crimes as it treats Nazi crimes in what it teaches, commemorates, honors and funds, without making belief in it, or praise of it, a crime.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Nazism|Nazism]]:** Nazism ranked human beings by supposed race and used the state to strip, expel, and kill those it ranked lowest. It murdered six million Jews and millions of others, including disabled people, Roma, Poles, and Soviet prisoners of war. I see it as the clearest case of what I reject: rights assigned by group identity instead of held by every sentient individual. Nazism is condemned at the same severity as communism, socialism, and Islam, without making belief in it a crime.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Islamism|Islamism]]:** Islamism is the political project of ruling by Islamic law. Where it holds power, it punishes apostasy and blasphemy, sometimes with death, and gives women and non-Muslims lesser legal standing. Its jihadist wing is the deadliest source of terrorism in the world today. It is ranked as dangerous as Nazism, and by violence it is the most dangerous ideology in the world today; communism, carried today mainly through cultural Marxism, is equally insidious and poisonous to civilization by its wide acceptance among the young and in universities ([[2. Political and Social Character#^cultural-marxism|Cultural Marxism]]). Islam, whose program it carries out, is condemned at the same severity as Nazism, communism, and socialism, without making belief in it a crime.

## [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties]]
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Individual Liberty vs State Power|Individual Liberty vs State Power]]:** The government should leave people alone unless they are actively harming others or about to do so.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]:** Emergency powers must be necessary, narrow, and temporary.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Government Structure and Checks|Government Structure and Checks]]:** Government exists to protect rights and must justify every expansion.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Term Limits|Term Limits]]:** I oppose term limits on any elected office.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Federalism|Federalism]]:** Defense, borders, and immigration should be federal, and schools should be local, with each state funding about \$10,000 per child that follows the child to any public school in the state.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Property Rights|Property Rights]]:** Once ownership is properly established, it is meant to last.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Self-Defense and Defense of Others|Self-Defense and Defense of Others]]:** People may use force to protect themselves or others anywhere, with no duty to retreat.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Freedom of Association|Freedom of Association]]:** People, clubs, and religious groups should be free to choose whom they associate with, but only so long as they violate no one's rights: joining or organizing a group to commit or plan rights violations, such as a conspiracy, gang, or organized violent group, ought not to be protected, and no one should be held in a group against their will.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Privacy Rights|Privacy Rights]]:** To me, privacy means control over access to one’s body, home, private spaces, and personal information.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Surveillance State|Surveillance State]]:** Facial recognition and social credit should be opt-in only.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Surveillance and Digital Technology|Surveillance and Digital Technology]]:** Data privacy should be respected and all data trading should be opt-in.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]:** Everyone should answer for what they do, and people with more power should answer to a higher standard.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Pretrial Detention and Bail|Pretrial Detention and Bail]]:** Defendants should be released before trial unless a judge finds they are dangerous or likely to flee.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Civil Asset Forfeiture|Civil Asset Forfeiture]]:** The state should not take property from people who have not been convicted, except assets of foreign cartels formally designated as war enemies or Foreign Terrorist Organizations, which are seized as enemy property on the combatant track, and true contraband, which may be seized and destroyed or returned to owners.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Role of Juries|Role of Juries]]:** Conviction should require a unanimous jury of at least twelve, in civil and criminal cases alike.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Voting and Elections|Voting and Elections]]:** Voters must show ID, mail-in ballots ought to be fully authenticated and encrypted, [[Glossary#Felony and misdemeanor|felons]] should not vote, and only net taxpayers should vote — a conflict of interest, because the franchise decides other people's money.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Election Administration|Election Administration]]:** Elections should require ID, authenticated mail ballots, and clean voter rolls, with money limited to individual donations.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Ranked-Choice Voting|Ranked-Choice Voting]]:** Ranked-choice voting should be required for every federal, state, and local election, by constitutional amendment.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Direct Democracy and Citizen Initiatives|Direct Democracy and Citizen Initiatives]]:** States and localities may offer citizen initiatives and referendums. A constitutional amendment creates a national initiative for federal statutes and amendments, reaching the ballot with signatures from 1% (statute) or 2% (amendment) of qualified voters within 18 months — and "qualified" means the same stack as every election: ID, knowledge exam, net taxpayer, no felons.

## [[4. Religion & Secularism]]
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Role of Religion in Government|Role of Religion in Government]]:** Religion should stay out of government, and officials should not turn scripture into law. You are free to be a moderate, practicing Muslim, but I would not allow a Muslim into public office or any official capacity, and the state should not endorse Islam: like adherents of Nazism, communism, and socialism, Muslims may not hold public office or any taxpayer-funded position, under the excluded-ideologies amendment ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^excluded-ideologies|Excluded ideologies]]). Every taxpayer-funded position should be a sworn position ([[4. Religion & Secularism#Oath of Office and Perjury|Oath of Office and Perjury]]): anyone seeking one swears an oath rejecting ideologies hostile to the country, and lying on it is perjury for seeking a taxpayer-funded position.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Religious Freedom|Religious Freedom]]:** Religious bodies and private people should choose their associations freely, and businesses may decline expressive work on conscience, but no business open to the public may refuse a person for a protected trait. You are free to be a moderate, practicing Muslim and practice in your home if you would like, but you cannot build mosques for a religion antithetical to Western cohesion; existing mosques may stay open but may not expand, relocate, or rebuild, are capped at today's number, and get no state support; other places that promote Islam fall under the same bar on building, rebuilding, expansion, or relocation as places that promote Nazism or communism ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^building-bar|Property Rights]]); and the Constitution should be amended to designate excluded ideologies, religious or secular, as dangerous to the fabric of the nation, covering Nazism, communism, socialism, and Islam on the same terms for the bars on building, rebuilding, expanding, or relocating places and on holding office ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^no-objection|Property Rights]]).
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Religious Exemptions|Religious Exemptions]]:** Religion should give no exemption from basic taxes.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Islam and Extremism|Islam and Extremism]]:** I see Islam as the most toxic, problematic major religion on earth by a lot. When Muslim numbers increase, the country moves further from a cohesive Western society built on the shared ideals America values first. I see Islamism as incompatible with Western society because of its record of rights violations.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Conflict, Persecution, and Hijra|Conflict, Persecution, and Hijra]]:** Islamic expansion has also come through migration and integration, Hijra, a way of taking over countries from within. Current events show this in practice: the uptick of Muslims in the EU, in office, and in the USA, with very clear trends of Muslim migration and a changing demographic.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Sharia as State Law|Sharia as State Law]]:** I protect private religious practice in the home. Sharia enforced as state law is not protected, because it punishes belief, speech, and consensual private conduct and gives people unequal legal standing by sex and religion. These rules violate negative rights whatever their source.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#The Islamic Faith in Practice|The Islamic Faith in Practice]]:** I oppose Islamism, and I am also strongly at odds with, and skeptical of, the Islamic faith as classically taught and widely practiced. Its mainstream legal tradition gives non-Muslims and women lesser standing and prescribes death for leaving the faith, and surveys show many Muslims still hold these views. My critique targets doctrines; the only consequences for any person are the two civic disabilities that follow adherence to an excluded ideology, never ancestry or identity. Muslims may practice privately in their homes, but no new mosques may be built, and existing mosques are capped at today's number with no expansion, relocation, rebuilding, or state support.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Ritual Slaughter and Animal Sacrifice|Ritual Slaughter and Animal Sacrifice]]:** Ritual slaughter and animal sacrifice should be banned outright for every faith.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Christianity and Western Heritage|Christianity and Western Heritage]]:** I view Western heritage and moderate Christianity as good, and I see a Western society shaped by moderate Christianity as culturally better than an anti-Western Islamic one.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Atheism, Secularism, and Faith|Atheism, Secularism, and Faith]]:** The government should stay neutral toward religions that respect rights.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Religious Upbringing and Children|Religious Upbringing and Children]]:** Parents should be able to raise children in their faith and choose religious schools or homeschooling.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#High-Control Groups|High-Control Groups]]:** No group should be punished for what it believes or teaches, and the two civic disabilities for [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^excluded-ideologies|excluded ideologies]], including the bar on building, rebuilding, expanding, or relocating places that promote anti-constitutional causes ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^building-bar|Property Rights]]), are not penalties, but force, fraud, coerced labor, and abuse ought to be prosecuted, leaders should answer for violations they direct, the head of the organization answers for its fines by default, and every member should be free to leave.

## [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance]]
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Economic System|Economic System]]:** **Markets work better than the alternatives for making people richer over time.** Countries and periods that protected private property and let prices and competition guide most decisions produced higher living standards, less extreme poverty, and more innovation than systems that put the state in charge of most production and prices.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Socialism|Socialism]]:** I see socialism in its core sense, social or state ownership of the means of production and planning in place of markets, as antithetical to my framework and a terrible idea, because it seizes property, forces redistribution, compels labor and speech, and suppresses dissent. Social democracy is different: the Nordic countries are market economies with large welfare states. I share the goal of a floor for basic needs but would fund only a minimal one and leave the rest to markets and voluntary giving.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Labor and Unions|Labor and Unions]]:** Unions should be voluntary and no one should have to join or pay one to work.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Antitrust|Antitrust]]:** Big is not illegal.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Taxation]]:** The main federal tax should be simple: most people below a clear income line pay nothing, and those with low earnings receive a top-up on what they earn. Parents should get a fully refundable credit per child that doubles from the third child ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#^family-credits|Births and Family Formation]]): since restricting immigration will lower the population, we want as many things as possible to help parents and raise birth rates to replacement level. I would replace today's payroll taxes with one visible transition levy, capped at 16% of wages, that pays off the benefits already promised and ends when they end.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Fiscal Policy|Fiscal Policy]]:** The budget should balance, deficits should be kept to a minimum, and the national debt should be paid down; on my model that means balance by 2050 with no household paying more than today.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Monetary Policy|Monetary Policy]]:** Balanced budgets keep money stable.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public-Sector Size and Civil Service|Public-Sector Size and Civil Service]]:** Government should be sized to its essential functions, with defense first.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public Funding of NGOs|Public Funding of NGOs]]:** The government ought to defund NGOs that run on public money: no grants, payment only under contracts with measurable deliverables, and no public money for advocacy.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Civil Service Protections|Civil Service Protections]]:** Hiring should be by merit.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Welfare and Entitlements|Welfare and Entitlements]]:** People already on benefits who followed the rules should keep their entitlement through a phase-out period; the funding behind it may change. Failing the net-taxpayer test suspends the vote ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^net-fiscal-vote|net fiscal voting bar]]).
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Child Support|Child Support]]:** Both genetic parents who consented to the sex owe support.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Births and Family Formation|Births and Family Formation]]:** America should get back above 2.1 children per woman through support that grows with family size, including a child credit that doubles from the third child, a birth credit for third and later children, and income-tax exemptions for mothers of three (half) and four (full) ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#^family-credits|family credits]]), plus cheaper housing through freed-up building including family-size units, childcare deregulation, and rising earnings for young adults, plus a higher-trust culture that helps young people marry, phone-free schools, childhood-independence laws, car-seat mandate review, cheaper first homes so young adults can leave home earlier, more places to meet in person, and shorter paths into careers, with no paid-leave mandate and no marriage penalty.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Social Security and Medicare Transition|Social Security and Medicare Transition]]:** Social Security and Medicare should become voluntary for new entrants. I would keep the promise to people already protected with a capped, temporary levy and spending cuts, not new debt.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Housing, Zoning, and Homelessness|Housing, Zoning, and Homelessness]]:** Housing should be left mainly to property owners and the market.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare|Healthcare]]:** People should choose between a publicly supported plan and private insurance.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval|Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval]]:** The public health option should be voluntary, with taxpayers funding it only by choice.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Private Education Oversight|Private Education Oversight]]:** Private and religious schools have the full right to exist and should be privately funded.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#K-12 Funding|K-12 Funding]]:** Each state should fund about \$10,000 per child per year for basic K-12 education, with a disability add-on set from actual need, following the child to any public school, district or charter, statewide; private and religious schools are paid by parents, and homeschooling families get a fully refundable \$1,000 tax credit per child.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Infrastructure and Regulation|Infrastructure and Regulation]]:** Taxes should pay to keep core infrastructure safe and working.

## [[6. Criminal Justice & Security]]
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Evidentiary Standard|Evidentiary Standard]]:** Nobody should be executed unless the proof is overwhelming and backed by objective evidence such as video and physical evidence, not just testimony.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]:** Deliberate murder should be punished by death, and so should violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, and selling or distributing hard drugs proven to the same strict standard.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Insanity Defense|Insanity Defense]]:** Mental illness does not undo a rights violation: a mentally handicapped person who kills still commits murder. "incompetent to stand trial" is usually applied too broadly; for the large majority of capital crime: arrest, convict, execute.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Lesser Homicide|Lesser Homicide]]:** A self-defense killing that went too far but was close to justified should be a lesser homicide with a lighter sentence set by the judge.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Human Trafficking|Human Trafficking]]:** I see human trafficking of adults or children, and kidnapping, as egregious rights violations that ought to be capital.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Age of Consent|Age of Consent]]:** The age of consent should be 18, except under the close-in-age exception below, and a minor cannot consent to sex with an adult.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Incest|Incest]]:** All sexual contact between blood relatives should be banned, even between consenting adults, as a misdemeanor punished by an income-scaled fine plus labor (one mark on a third offense) and a precaution against reproduction, since any child conceived would have to be carried to term; a parent or step-parent cannot validly consent with their adult child, and blood relatives may not marry.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life|Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life]]:** Repeat offenders who keep committing crimes after being punished should lose their right to life once they reach three marks, provided they are still active. At least one earlier mark must fall within the ten years before the new crime.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Income-Scaled Fines|Income-Scaled Fines]]:** All fines, criminal, civil, and regulatory, should be scaled to income and wealth, always paired with labor for individuals, with corporations paying up to 4% of global revenue, or their gain from the offense if higher, and the CEO personally responsible as the one ultimately responsible for the company's actions, so no designated fault-officer can take the fall.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor|Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor]]:** No one should be imprisoned for life with nothing to do.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row|Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row]]:** Capital trials should take 30 days at most, and the sentence should be carried out the day it is handed down.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Public Executions|Public Executions]]:** Public executions should return, carried out in public or openly recorded so citizens can see what the state does in their name, with hanging and gunshot both acceptable methods.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment|Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment]]:** Studies of today's slow, uncertain system cannot show whether a swift, certain death penalty deters, and the case for it rests on desert, incapacitation and forfeiture.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Wrongful Convictions and Compensation|Wrongful Convictions and Compensation]]:** Convicting an innocent person should be treated as the state violating their rights.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Juvenile Offenders|Juvenile Offenders]]:** There should be no fixed age of criminal responsibility; how severely a minor is punished should depend on how serious the crime was.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Civil Commitment|Civil Commitment]]:** No one should be confined for being homeless or mentally ill.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Statutes of Limitations|Statutes of Limitations]]:** Murder, violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, human trafficking, and selling or distributing hard drugs should be prosecutable no matter how much time has passed, as long as the strict evidence standard is met.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice|Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice]]:** The justice system is first about protecting victims and the public and giving offenders what they deserve; reconciling offenders with victims or reintegrating them comes second and should never change the sentence.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Victims' Rights|Victims' Rights]]:** Offenders lose their rights when they violate others'.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police Power and Accountability|Police Power and Accountability]]:** Police should get more money, more officers, and more respect, but officers who break the law should be punished more harshly than ordinary people.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police and Law Enforcement|Police and Law Enforcement]]:** Body cameras should be required everywhere, the worst crime areas should get militarized enforcement under zero tolerance, and theft and burglary should be treated severely.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Drug Policy|Drug Policy]]:** Using drugs privately should be legal, but not while a minor is present or while impaired around one; using them in public should be banned, and selling or distributing hard drugs should be a capital crime, with hard drugs obtained only through licensed clinical supply or personal production, never from a shop.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Border Security and Immigration Enforcement|Border Security and Immigration Enforcement]]:** Illegal immigrants should be removed by force, the border ought to be heavily fortified, and lethal force should be allowed when necessary.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Terrorism and Domestic Extremism]]:** Domestic terrorism should be treated as a serious threat.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Political Violence]]:** The 2020 riots were left-wing political violence that terrorism counts leave out, and both sides' violence is condemned equally.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Race and Crime]]:** I acknowledge a great disproportion in violence and crime between Black Americans and others. This is likely due to a lack of family structure and a generally corrosive culture that glorifies the wrong aspects of what creates a lasting society: disdain for education, aspiration for gangs, disrespect for peers, seeing crime as virtuous, and a permanent victim mentality with no accountability. [[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Intelligence, Race, and Crime|Gaps in tested ability]] also track much of the gap, and whether that gap is itself environmental, partly genetic, or both is not yet settled.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Gun Crime and Violence]]:** Gang and high-crime areas should get heavy, hot-spot policing and fast, harsh crackdowns, and high-rate violent offenders should be locked up, with full due process. El Salvador-model sweeps are justified only in a declared, time-limited emergency over riots, grave danger to the nation, or completely rampant crime; outside one, full due process applies ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^emergency-capital|Emergency Powers]]).
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Domestic Violence|Domestic Violence]]:** Extreme violence, intentional neglect, beating without drawing blood, and withholding food, water, or shelter as punishment against a spouse or family member should be capital if they cause lasting injury. Otherwise they fall in the grave tier, so they count as three marks at once: execution, experimentation, or life with labor.

## [[7. Cultural & Social Issues]]
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Family and Gender|Family and Gender]]:** The healthiest family is close to the traditional nuclear family, and I see traditional roles as best, but nothing should be mandated.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Feminism and Sex Equality|Feminism and Sex Equality]]:** Both sexes hold equal rights and should be judged by the same rules.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Marriage Law|Marriage Law]]:** Marriage should be a legal contract between consenting adults, same-sex couples included.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Abortion and Bioethics|Abortion and Bioethics]]:** I judge abortion by sentience, but I support pro-life policy in full because permissive laws can go further, and limits in some countries have moved later over time.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Assisted Death|Assisted Death]]:** Competent adults may choose assisted death with a willing doctor, after an independent capacity check.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#LGBTQ+ and Identity Issues|LGBTQ+ and Identity Issues]]:** Same-sex marriage should be legal, though I see male-female pairing as best.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Gender Identity in Schools and Institutions|Gender Identity in Schools and Institutions]]:** Public schools should stay out of gender-identity advocacy, never socially transition students, and never keep it from parents.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Curriculum Content|Curriculum Content]]:** History and civics should be taught unmodified, with more curriculum on taxes, government, how society works, and jobs.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Free Speech and Censorship|Free Speech and Censorship]]:** Speech should be fully protected except for true threats and incitement to imminent violence.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Pornography and Minors' Access|Pornography and Minors' Access]]:** Adult pornography made with consent should be legal.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Minors and Social Media|Minors and Social Media]]:** Following Australia's model, minors under 16 should not hold social media accounts; going one step further than Australia, 16- to 18-year-olds should have access only with parental opt-in.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Race and Diversity|Race and Diversity]]:** I support no quotas, no DEI preferences, and only merit in hiring and admissions, with narrow carve-outs ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#^merit-carveouts|Merit-rule carve-outs]]).
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Equal Treatment in Employment and Business|Equal Treatment in Employment and Business]]:** Employers and businesses, public or private, may not treat anyone better or worse for race, sex, or sexual orientation: no firing for being gay, no firing a transgender worker for traits or conduct that would be accepted in the other sex, and no quotas or DEI preferences, with narrow carve-outs ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#^merit-carveouts|Merit-rule carve-outs]]).
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Gun Rights|Gun Rights]]:** Qualified citizens should be able to buy guns, with background checks and red flag laws aimed at stopping bad actors.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Drugs and Vice|Drugs and Vice]]:** Marijuana should be legal and hard drugs legal for private, personal use, with no drug, marijuana included, used in public or around minors, selling or distributing hard drugs capital, prostitution legal and regulated, and adults free to gamble.

Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]:** I see a culturally similar society as strongest, so immigration should be limited to people who fit Western values and assimilate, with American labor needs as a secondary criterion. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship|Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship]]:** Green card and visa holders should be removed, after a court finding by clear and convincing evidence, if they take cash welfare, SNAP, housing aid, non-emergency Medicaid, or the last-resort allowance, fail the naturalization English and civics test within two years, or are convicted of any crime above a basic infraction.

Foreign Policy & International Relations#Israel and the Middle East|West Bank]]:** The West Bank's armed threat should be forcibly removed and the territory walled off from Israel entirely, since a negotiated middle ground is unlikely; those individually found hostile by conduct who will not assimilate should be forced out; under current polling, entry from the West Bank and Gaza should be banned completely.

## [[9. Energy & Environment]]
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Climate Change|Climate Change]]:** Climate change is real and nearly all recent warming is human-caused (IPCC), but it is not apocalyptic.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Adaptation and Disaster Policy|Adaptation and Disaster Policy]]:** I see climate adaptation as practical.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Energy Mix|Energy Mix]]:** Fossil fuels should stay where they work best while renewables grow through private innovation.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Energy Policy|Energy Policy]]:** Any shift toward cleaner energy should be slow and market-driven, with no mandates and no source forced to shut down early.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Grid Reliability and Permitting|Grid Reliability and Permitting]]:** Reliability comes first, no source should be forced off early, and permitting should be streamlined while environmental protections stay.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Regulation and Conservation|Regulation and Conservation]]:** Environmental rules should protect sanctuary lands and herbivore populations, and pollution should be regulated to keep people healthy.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Conservation vs Development|Conservation vs Development]]:** Nature should be preserved where rights are not violated.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Pollution and Liability|Pollution and Liability]]:** Pollution that harms people violates their rights.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Subsidies and Mandates|Subsidies and Mandates]]:** I support no green-tech subsidies unless taxpayers opt in, and no mandates.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#International Climate Agreements|International Climate Agreements]]:** I welcome awareness efforts, and I see ending animal agriculture as the ideal contribution.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Nuclear Power|Nuclear Power]]:** Nuclear power should expand because it is proven safe, with strict safety standards.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Nuclear Waste and Fuel Cycle|Nuclear Waste and Fuel Cycle]]:** Nuclear power should expand, spent fuel should be stored safely, recycling can develop, and storage sites ought to go in low-density areas.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Water Resources and Rights|Water Resources and Rights]]:** Water rights are property, pollution of water is a violation, and markets should allocate water within health standards.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Agriculture, Grazing, and Habitat|Agriculture, Grazing, and Habitat]]:** Animal agriculture should be banned outright and shut down immediately, with no wind-down.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Sanctuary Plan]]:** Every farmed, fished, breeding-mill, and test animal alive at the shutdown should be kept alive and given sanctuary for its natural life. Most go to sanctuaries on converted feedlots and farms, run by the state only in the emergency phase and then by licensed nonprofits, and animals that are safe to keep may be adopted after strict vetting. Firms that earn most of their revenue from animal agriculture convert fully or are liquidated, with no compensation to shareholders, and the proceeds after creditors go to the sanctuaries. Those proceeds, sanctuary bonds and a flat Sanctuary Levy of no more than 3% pay for it, and the levy ends when the sanctuary population falls below 1% of its starting animal units and the bonds are repaid. The uncompensated industry transfers and the Sanctuary Levy are a named, time-limited property exception justified by the animals' negative rights ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^sanctuary-property-exception|Sanctuary property exception]]).
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Agriculture and Food Policy|Agriculture and Food Policy]]:** I support no farm subsidies without opt-in funding, food safety to health standards, and lab-grown meat, the goal and the replacement for animal agriculture, allowed to compete and scale up over time.
- **[[9. Energy & Environment#Commercial Fishing and Aquaculture|Commercial Fishing and Aquaculture]]:** Commercial fishing and fish farming of sentient animals should be banned outright and shut down immediately, with no wind-down.
