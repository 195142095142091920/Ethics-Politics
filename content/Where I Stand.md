## [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework]]
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Conflicts Between Rights-Holders|Conflicts Between Rights-Holders]]:** I believe full moral agency needs sentience plus intentional action and foresight, and that it scales with sophistication.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Applications elsewhere|Applications elsewhere]]:** I believe animal agriculture and aquaculture should be banned outright and instantly; any gradual change in the real world is an aside, like taxes or education.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Stoic Eudaimonism|Stoic Eudaimonism]]:** I use Stoic eudaimonism as my personal operating system for character: focus on what is within my control, practice wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance, and keep composure while acting decisively.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Limits of Politics|Limits of Politics]]:** I believe the state exists to protect negative rights and the security of the community that sustains them, not to equalize outcomes.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Corporations as Moral Entities|Corporations as Moral Entities]]:** I see corporations as tools, not moral agents.
- **[[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Duties to Future Generations|Duties to Future Generations]]:** I believe present agents may not impose extreme, unjustified risk of death or rights destruction on future sentient beings.

## [[2. Political and Social Character]]

- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Western Values and Compatibility|Western Values and Compatibility]]:** To me, "Western" means a set of values, not an ethnicity or a place: rights, liberty, religious freedom with church-state separation, rule of law, civic responsibility, and opportunity. I believe being Muslim fails the values test.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Economic Orientation|Economic Orientation]]:** I support free markets, a flat tax with a large zero bracket, opt-in welfare, no business subsidies without opt-in, regulation that must justify itself, and balanced budgets with defense first.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Cultural Stance|Cultural Stance]]:** I prefer traditional family life as the default but not as a legal mandate. Merit should replace identity politics, speech should be near-absolute, and the government should condemn Islamism and communism without punishing belief.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Order and Security|Order and Security]]:** I believe crime should be met hard: a mandatory death penalty for murder (including attempted murder), violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and human trafficking, forfeiture for repeat offenders, and decisive action against cartels, under due process and police oversight outside emergencies, with no penalty for belief.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#The Future and Technology|The Future and Technology]]:** I embrace technology within sentient-rights limits: I support AI and genetics, data should be opt-in, animal testing should be banned, and sentient AI should have rights and the same forfeiture rules as everyone else.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Relation to Other Political Traditions|Relation to Other Political Traditions]]:** I share the liberal and libertarian commitment to rights, property, and markets, but I accept far more force against criminals and at the border.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Communism in Practice|Communism in Practice]]:** Communist theory promises a classless society in which "the free development of each is the condition for the free development of all." In practice, every state that tried to build it abolished private property by force, banned rival parties, and ran forced-labor camps, and several caused famines and mass killings that cost millions of lives. Communism is condemned at the same severity as Nazism and Islamism, without making belief in it a crime.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Nazism|Nazism]]:** Nazism ranked human beings by supposed race and used the state to strip, expel, and kill those it ranked lowest. It murdered six million Jews and millions of others, including disabled people, Roma, Poles, and Soviet prisoners of war. I see it as the clearest case of what I reject: rights assigned by group identity instead of held by every sentient individual. Nazism is condemned at the same severity as communism and Islamism, without making belief in it a crime.
- **[[2. Political and Social Character#Islamism|Islamism]]:** Islamism is the political project of ruling by Islamic law. Where it holds power, it punishes apostasy and blasphemy, sometimes with death, and gives women and non-Muslims lesser legal standing. Its jihadist wing is the deadliest source of terrorism in the world today. It is ranked as dangerous as Nazism and as the most dangerous ideology in the world today, and condemned at the same severity as Nazism and communism, without making belief in it a crime.

## [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties]]
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Individual Liberty vs State Power|Individual Liberty vs State Power]]:** The government should leave people alone unless they are actively harming others or about to do so.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]:** I believe emergency powers must be necessary, narrow, and temporary.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Government Structure and Checks|Government Structure and Checks]]:** I believe government exists to protect rights and must justify every expansion.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Term Limits|Term Limits]]:** I oppose term limits on any elected office.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Federalism|Federalism]]:** I believe defense, borders, and immigration should be federal, and schools should be local, with each state funding about \$10,000 per child that follows the child to any public school in the state.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Property Rights|Property Rights]]:** I believe that once ownership is properly established, it is meant to last.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Self-Defense and Defense of Others|Self-Defense and Defense of Others]]:** I believe people may use force to protect themselves or others anywhere, with no duty to retreat.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Freedom of Association|Freedom of Association]]:** I believe people, clubs, and religious groups should be free to choose whom they associate with, but only so long as they violate no one's rights: joining or organizing a group to commit or plan rights violations, such as a conspiracy, gang, or organized violent group, ought not to be protected, and no one should be held in a group against their will.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Privacy Rights|Privacy Rights]]:** To me, privacy means control over access to one’s body, home, private spaces, and personal information.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Surveillance State|Surveillance State]]:** I believe facial recognition and social credit should be opt-in only.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]:** I believe everyone should answer for what they do, and people with more power should answer to a higher standard.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Pretrial Detention and Bail|Pretrial Detention and Bail]]:** I believe defendants should be released before trial unless a judge finds they are dangerous or likely to flee.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Civil Asset Forfeiture|Civil Asset Forfeiture]]:** I believe the state should not take property from people who have not been convicted, except assets of foreign cartels formally designated as war enemies or Foreign Terrorist Organizations, which are seized as enemy property on the combatant track, and true contraband, which may be seized and destroyed or returned to owners.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Role of Juries|Role of Juries]]:** I believe conviction should require a unanimous jury of at least twelve, in civil and criminal cases alike.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Voting and Elections|Voting and Elections]]:** I believe voters must show ID, mail-in ballots ought to be fully authenticated and encrypted, and [[Glossary#Felony and misdemeanor|felons]] should not vote.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Election Administration|Election Administration]]:** I believe elections should require ID, authenticated mail ballots, and clean voter rolls, with money limited to individual donations.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Ranked-Choice Voting|Ranked-Choice Voting]]:** I believe ranked-choice voting should be required for every federal, state, and local election, by constitutional amendment.
- **[[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Direct Democracy and Citizen Initiatives|Direct Democracy and Citizen Initiatives]]:** I believe states and localities may offer citizen initiatives and referendums. I support a constitutional amendment creating a national initiative for federal statutes and amendments, reaching the ballot with signatures from 1% (statute) or 2% (amendment) of qualified voters within 18 months.

- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Islam and Extremism|Islam and Extremism]]:** I see Islam as the most toxic, problematic major religion on earth by a lot, and more Muslims in a Western country brings no good outcomes. I see Islamism as incompatible with Western society because of its record of rights violations.
- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Conflict, Persecution, and Hijra|Conflict, Persecution, and Hijra]]:** I believe Islamic expansion has also come through migration and integration, Hijra, a way of taking over countries from within. I believe current events show this in practice: the uptick of Muslims in the EU, in office, and in the USA, with very clear trends of Muslim migration and a changing demographic.

These rules violate negative rights whatever their source.

- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#Atheism, Secularism, and Faith|Atheism, Secularism, and Faith]]:** I believe the government should stay neutral toward religions that respect rights.

- **[[4. Religion & Secularism#High-Control Groups|High-Control Groups]]:** I believe no group should be restricted for what it believes or teaches, but force, fraud, coerced labor, and abuse ought to be prosecuted, leaders should answer for violations they direct, the head of the organization answers for its fines by default, and every member should be free to leave.

## [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance]]
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Economic System|Economic System]]:** **I believe markets work better than the alternatives for making people richer over time.** Countries and periods that protected private property and let prices and competition guide most decisions produced higher living standards, less extreme poverty, and more innovation than systems that put the state in charge of most production and prices.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Socialism|Socialism]]:** I see socialism in its core sense, social or state ownership of the means of production and planning in place of markets, as antithetical to my framework and a terrible idea, because it seizes property, forces redistribution, compels labor and speech, and suppresses dissent. Social democracy is different: the Nordic countries are market economies with large welfare states. I share the goal of a floor for basic needs but would fund only a minimal one and leave the rest to markets and voluntary giving.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Labor and Unions|Labor and Unions]]:** I believe unions should be voluntary and no one should have to join or pay one to work.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Antitrust|Antitrust]]:** I believe big is not illegal.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Taxation]]:** I believe the main federal tax should be simple: most people below a clear income line pay nothing, and those with low earnings receive a top-up on what they earn. I believe parents should get a fully refundable \$1,500 credit per child, \$3,000 from the third child, because since restricting immigration will lower the population, we want as many things as possible to help parents and raise birth rates to replacement level. I would replace today's payroll taxes with one visible transition levy, capped at 16% of wages, that pays off the benefits already promised and ends when they end.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Fiscal Policy|Fiscal Policy]]:** I believe the budget should balance, deficits should be kept to a minimum, and the national debt should be paid down; on my model that means balance by 2050 with no household paying more than today.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Monetary Policy|Monetary Policy]]:** I believe balanced budgets keep money stable.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public-Sector Size and Civil Service|Public-Sector Size and Civil Service]]:** I believe government should be sized to its essential functions, with defense first.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Civil Service Protections|Civil Service Protections]]:** I believe hiring should be by merit.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Welfare and Entitlements|Welfare and Entitlements]]:** I believe people already on benefits who followed the rules should keep their entitlement through a phase-out period; the funding behind it may change.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Child Support|Child Support]]:** I believe both genetic parents who consented to the sex owe support.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Births and Family Formation|Births and Family Formation]]:** I believe America should get back above 2.1 children per woman through support that grows with family size, including a \$3,000 credit from the third child, a \$5,000 birth credit for third and later children, and income-tax exemptions for mothers of three (half) and four (full), plus cheaper housing through freed-up building including family-size units, childcare deregulation, and rising earnings for young adults, plus a higher-trust culture that helps young people marry, phone-free schools, childhood-independence laws, car-seat mandate review, cheaper first homes so young adults can leave home earlier, more places to meet in person, and shorter paths into careers, with no paid-leave mandate and no marriage penalty.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Social Security and Medicare Transition|Social Security and Medicare Transition]]:** I believe Social Security and Medicare should become voluntary for new entrants. I would keep the promise to people already protected with a capped, temporary levy and spending cuts, not new debt.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Housing, Zoning, and Homelessness|Housing, Zoning, and Homelessness]]:** I believe housing should be left mainly to property owners and the market.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare|Healthcare]]:** I believe people should choose between a publicly supported plan and private insurance.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval|Healthcare Funding, Emergency Care, and Drug Approval]]:** I believe the public health option should be voluntary, with taxpayers funding it only by choice.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Private Education Oversight|Private Education Oversight]]:** I believe private and religious schools have the full right to exist and should be privately funded.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#K-12 Funding|K-12 Funding]]:** I believe each state should fund about \$10,000 per child per year for basic K-12 education, with a disability add-on set from actual need, following the child to any public school, district or charter, statewide; private and religious schools are paid by parents, and homeschooling families get a fully refundable \$1,000 tax credit per child. I believe that since restricting immigration will lower the population, we want as many things as possible to help parents and raise birth rates to replacement level.
- **[[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Infrastructure and Regulation|Infrastructure and Regulation]]:** I believe taxes should pay to keep core infrastructure safe and working.

## [[6. Criminal Justice & Security]]
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Evidentiary Standard|Evidentiary Standard]]:** I believe nobody should be executed unless the proof is overwhelming and backed by objective evidence such as video and physical evidence, not just testimony.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]:** I believe deliberate murder should be punished by death, and so should violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and human trafficking proven to the same strict standard.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Insanity Defense|Insanity Defense]]:** I believe mental illness does not undo a rights violation: a mentally handicapped person who kills still commits murder. I believe "incompetent to stand trial" is usually applied too broadly; for the large majority of capital crime: arrest, convict, execute.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Lesser Homicide|Lesser Homicide]]:** I believe a self-defense killing that went too far but was close to justified should be a lesser homicide with a lighter sentence set by the judge.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Human Trafficking|Human Trafficking]]:** I see human trafficking of adults or children, and kidnapping, as egregious rights violations that ought to be capital.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Age of Consent|Age of Consent]]:** I believe the age of consent should be 18, except under the close-in-age exception below, and a minor cannot consent to sex with an adult.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Incest|Incest]]:** I believe all sexual contact between blood relatives should be banned, even between consenting adults, as a misdemeanor punished by an income-scaled fine plus labor (one mark on a third offense) and a precaution against reproduction, since any child conceived would have to be carried to term; a parent or step-parent cannot validly consent with their adult child, and blood relatives may not marry.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life|Repeat Offenders and Forfeiture of the Right to Life]]:** I believe repeat offenders who keep committing crimes after being punished should lose their right to life once they reach three marks, provided they are still active. At least one earlier mark must fall within the ten years before the new crime.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Income-Scaled Fines|Income-Scaled Fines]]:** I believe all fines, criminal, civil, and regulatory, should be scaled to income and wealth, always paired with labor for individuals, with corporations paying up to 4% of global revenue, or their gain from the offense if higher, and the CEO personally responsible as the one ultimately responsible for the company's actions, so no designated fault-officer can take the fall.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor|Abolition of Permanent Incarceration and Compelled Labor]]:** I believe no one should be imprisoned for life with nothing to do.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row|Capital Trials and the Abolition of Death Row]]:** I believe capital trials should take 30 days at most, and the sentence should be carried out the day it is handed down.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Public Executions|Public Executions]]:** I believe public executions should return, carried out in public or openly recorded so citizens can see what the state does in their name, with hanging and gunshot both acceptable methods.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment|Deterrence and the Justification for Capital Punishment]]:** I accept that studies cannot say whether the death penalty deters, partly because the current system is so slow and uncertain.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Wrongful Convictions and Compensation|Wrongful Convictions and Compensation]]:** I believe convicting an innocent person should be treated as the state violating their rights.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Juvenile Offenders|Juvenile Offenders]]:** I believe there should be no fixed age of criminal responsibility; how severely a minor is punished should depend on how serious the crime was.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Civil Commitment|Civil Commitment]]:** I believe no one should be confined for being homeless or mentally ill.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Statutes of Limitations|Statutes of Limitations]]:** I believe murder, violent rape, child molestation, child rape, kidnapping, and human trafficking should be prosecutable no matter how much time has passed, as long as the strict evidence standard is met.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice|Restorative Justice Secondary to Justice]]:** I believe the justice system is first about protecting victims and the public and giving offenders what they deserve; reconciling offenders with victims or reintegrating them comes second and should never change the sentence.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Victims' Rights|Victims' Rights]]:** I believe offenders lose their rights when they violate others'.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police Power and Accountability|Police Power and Accountability]]:** I believe police should get more money, more officers, and more respect, but officers who break the law should be punished more harshly than ordinary people.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Police and Law Enforcement|Police and Law Enforcement]]:** I believe police should get more money, more officers, and more respect, and officers who break the law should be punished more than ordinary citizens.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Drug Policy|Drug Policy]]:** I believe using drugs privately should not be a crime, but using them in public should be banned.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Border Security and Immigration Enforcement|Border Security and Immigration Enforcement]]:** I believe illegal immigrants should be removed by force, the border ought to be heavily fortified, and lethal force should be allowed when necessary.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Terrorism and Domestic Extremism]]:** I believe domestic terrorism should be treated as a serious threat.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Terrorism and Domestic Extremism|Political Violence]]:** The 2020 riots were left-wing political violence that terrorism counts leave out, and both sides' violence is condemned equally.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Race and Crime]]:** I acknowledge a great disproportion in violence and crime between Black Americans and others. I believe this is likely due to a lack of family structure and a generally corrosive culture that glorifies the wrong aspects of what creates a lasting society: disdain for education, aspiration for gangs, disrespect for peers, seeing crime as virtuous, and a permanent victim mentality with no accountability. Gaps in tested ability also track much of the gap, and whether that gap is itself environmental, partly genetic, or both is not yet settled.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Gun Crime and Violence|Gun Crime and Violence]]:** I believe gang and high-crime areas should get heavy, hot-spot policing and fast, harsh crackdowns, and high-rate violent offenders should be locked up, with full due process. I believe El Salvador-model sweeps are justified in times of emergency, in times of grave danger to the nation, or in hot spots with completely rampant crime.
- **[[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Domestic Violence|Domestic Violence]]:** I believe extreme violence, intentional neglect, beating without drawing blood, and withholding food, water, or shelter as punishment against a spouse or family member should be capital if they cause lasting injury. Otherwise they are egregious: three marks at once: execution, experimentation, or life with labor.

Cultural & Social Issues#Equal Treatment in Employment and Business|Equal Treatment in Employment and Business]]:** I believe employers and businesses, public or private, may not treat anyone better or worse for race, sex, or sexual orientation: no firing for being gay, no firing a transgender worker for traits or conduct that would be accepted in the other sex, and no quotas or DEI preferences.

- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Patriotism and National Identity|Patriotism and National Identity]]:** I believe a cohesive, patriotic culture is best, and multiculturalism works only with shared values.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Language and Assimilation|Language and Assimilation]]:** I believe assimilation is paramount and English should be the shared civic language.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Evidence: Herbivorization|Evidence: Herbivorization]]:** I believe deliberately killing a sentient animal outside of self-defense is murder, graded by the animal's sentience: high-sentience animals should be on the capital track, and killing lower-sentience animals should still be punishable and count as marks against the offender.
- **[[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Animal Cruelty Law|Animal Cruelty Law]]:** I define animal cruelty as extreme violence, intentional neglect, beating without drawing blood, or withholding food, water, or shelter as deliberate punishment.

## [[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations]]
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Interventionism vs Non-Intervention|Interventionism vs Non-Intervention]]:** I believe the U.S. should intervene where its safety is at stake, especially against Iran.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Alliances and NATO|Alliances and NATO]]:** I believe the U.S. should join alliances only when they make the country safer and never give up its sovereignty.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Trade and Economics|Trade and Economics]]:** I believe American manufacturing should come first, imports should come with matching exports, and tariffs should be treated cautiously.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]:** I see a culturally similar society as strongest, so I believe immigration should be limited to people who fit Western values and assimilate, with American labor needs as a secondary criterion. I want less immigration from Muslim-majority countries and am fine with banning Muslim immigration outright.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Investor Visas|Investor Visas]]:** I believe status should never be sold.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship|Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship]]:** I believe green card and visa holders should be removed, after a court finding by clear and convincing evidence, if they take cash welfare, SNAP, housing aid, non-emergency Medicaid, or the last-resort allowance, fail the naturalization English and civics test within two years, or are convicted of any crime above a basic infraction.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Mexico and Latin America|Mexico and Latin America]]:** I believe cartels should be treated as terrorists and destroyed, ideally together with the local government.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Israel and the Middle East|Israel and the Middle East]]:** I back Israel as the front line against Iran and terror.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Israel and the Middle East|Hamas]]:** Hamas is condemned as a terrorist organization and destroyed along with the Iranian regime's other allies in state-sponsored, anti-Western terror.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Israel and the Middle East|Two-State Solution and Gaza]]:** I believe a two-state solution is not viable and Gaza should likely be dissolved, since it has proven time and again hostile and uncooperative toward Israel.
- **[[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Israel and the Middle East|West Bank]]:** I believe the West Bank's armed threat should be forcibly removed and the territory walled off from Israel entirely, since a negotiated middle ground is unlikely; those who will not assimilate and remain hostile should be forced out.
