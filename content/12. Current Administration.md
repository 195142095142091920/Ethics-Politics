## Core Principles
This page grades the current presidential administration's major actions against the framework. Donald J. Trump took office for his second term on January 20, 2025. Each action is graded on direction as well as detail, because no administration will match the framework exactly: **Aligned** (direction and details match), **Directionally aligned (details differ)** (moves the way the framework does, by a different route or to a different degree), **Mixed** (parts point both ways, or the site has no settled position), **Directionally opposed** (moves against the framework, with some overlap), and **Opposed**. Grades reflect my framework, set out in the sections linked from each entry; descriptions are drawn from the official text of each action.

## Immigration and Borders

>[!tldr] In short
> 6 actions graded: 1 Aligned, 3 Directionally aligned (details differ), 1 Mixed, 1 Directionally opposed.

#### Interior enforcement and removal priorities
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14159 directs stepped-up interior enforcement, detention, and removal of people unlawfully present.

The framework removes illegal immigrants forcefully ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). It also requires a court finding and due process in each case, which the order leaves to existing procedure.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14159: Protecting the American People Against Invasion](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/29/2025-02006/protecting-the-american-people-against-invasion)

#### Birthright citizenship order
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14160 directs agencies not to recognize citizenship for children born to parents who are unlawfully present or in temporary status.

The framework also ends birthright citizenship, but by constitutional amendment and going forward only, without affecting anyone who gained citizenship under prior law ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). The direction matches; the vehicle and the retroactive reach differ.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14160: Protecting the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/29/2025-02007/protecting-the-meaning-and-value-of-american-citizenship)

#### Limits on universal injunctions
**June 27, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

In *Trump v. CASA*, arising from challenges to Executive Order 14160, the Supreme Court held that federal courts generally may not issue universal injunctions.

The ruling is procedural. The framework keeps court review of executive action ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]), and the ruling narrows the remedy without removing that review.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Supreme Court of the United States – [Trump v. CASA, Inc. (2025)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/24a884_8n59.pdf)

#### Refugee admissions suspended
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14163 suspends the U.S. Refugee Admissions Program pending review.

The framework treats admission as a privilege granted in the national interest, so a pause matches its direction ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]). It differs in detail: the framework still bars returning anyone to persecution or torture and uses safe third countries ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Removal of Non-Citizen Residents and Loss of Citizenship|Removal of Non-Citizen Residents]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14163: Realigning the United States Refugee Admissions Program](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/30/2025-02011/realigning-the-united-states-refugee-admissions-program)

#### Gold Card visa
**September 19, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally opposed**

Executive Order 14351 creates an expedited immigrant-visa path for a \$1 million gift to the Department of Commerce by an individual.

The framework admits people by compatibility with Western values and individual vetting, not by wealth, and never sells status ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Investor Visas|Investor Visas]]). Vetting still applies under the order, which is why the grade is directional rather than full opposition.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14351: The Gold Card](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/24/2025-18602/the-gold-card)

#### Ending birth tourism
**August 6, 2026** · **Grade: Aligned**

Executive Order 14419 restricts nonimmigrant entry for the purpose of giving birth in the United States to obtain citizenship for the child.

This matches the framework's goal of tying citizenship to lawful membership rather than place of birth ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Immigration and Borders|Immigration and Borders]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14419: Ending Birth Tourism](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/11/2026-16404/ending-birth-tourism)

## Crime, Justice, and Policing

>[!tldr] In short
> 7 actions graded: 1 Aligned, 4 Directionally aligned (details differ), 1 Mixed, 1 Opposed.

#### Death penalty restored as federal policy
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14164 directs the Attorney General to seek the death penalty in appropriate federal cases and to support states that carry it out.

The framework goes further: death is mandatory for murder and other capital crimes proven by clear-cut objective evidence ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]). Federal prosecutors keep discretion under the order.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14164: Restoring the Death Penalty and Protecting Public Safety](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/30/2025-02012/restoring-the-death-penalty-and-protecting-public-safety)

#### DC crime emergency
**August 11, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14333 declares a crime emergency in the District of Columbia and directs federal use of the Metropolitan Police Department.

Heavier policing matches the framework ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Core Principles|Criminal Justice]]). The framework requires every emergency to expire automatically on a fixed date unless renewed by the legislature ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14333: Declaring a Crime Emergency in the District of Columbia](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/14/2025-15550/declaring-a-crime-emergency-in-the-district-of-columbia)

#### Ending cashless bail
**August 25, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

Executive Order 14342 directs federal steps against jurisdictions that release defendants without cash bail.

The framework shares the aim of detaining dangerous defendants, but sets detention by an individual finding of danger or flight risk, never by ability to pay ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Pretrial Detention and Bail|Pretrial Detention and Bail]]). Cash bail is the mechanism the framework rejects.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14342: Taking Steps To End Cashless Bail To Protect Americans](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/28/2025-16618/taking-steps-to-end-cashless-bail-to-protect-americans)

#### Prosecuting flag burning
**August 25, 2025** · **Grade: Opposed**

Executive Order 14341 directs prosecution of flag burning where it can be charged under existing law, such as property or public-order offenses.

The framework protects speech except true threats and incitement ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Free Speech and Censorship|Free Speech and Censorship]]). Burning one's own flag harms no one's rights; damage to others' property is already a crime without the order.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14341: Prosecuting Burning of the American Flag](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/28/2025-16616/prosecuting-burning-of-the-american-flag)

#### Civil commitment for street disorder
**July 24, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14321 encourages civil commitment of people with serious mental illness who live on the streets or pose a risk, and enforcement against public camping and drug use.

Enforcement against public drug use matches the framework ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Drugs and Vice|Drugs and Vice]]). The framework also allows commitment, but only on a judicial finding of danger to others or lost capacity, never for homelessness alone, with outpatient treatment first and review every six months ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Civil Commitment|Civil Commitment]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14321: Ending Crime and Disorder on America's Streets](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/07/29/2025-14391/ending-crime-and-disorder-on-americas-streets)

#### Fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction
**December 15, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14367 designates illicit fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction for federal enforcement purposes.

Trafficking that kills fits the framework's severe punishment of acts that put others at risk of death ([[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty|Murder, Rape, and the Mandatory Death Penalty]]). The framework keeps personal use decriminalized ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Drugs and Vice|Drugs and Vice]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14367: Designating Fentanyl as a Weapon of Mass Destruction](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/18/2025-23417/designating-fentanyl-as-a-weapon-of-mass-destruction)

#### Second Amendment review
**February 7, 2025** · **Grade: Aligned**

Executive Order 14206 directs a review of federal actions that may infringe Second Amendment rights.

Matches the framework's gun-rights position ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Gun Rights|Gun Rights]]).

## Economy, Taxes, and Trade

>[!tldr] In short
> 3 actions graded: 1 Directionally aligned (details differ), 1 Mixed, 1 Directionally opposed.

#### Reciprocal tariffs
**April 2, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally opposed**

Executive Order 14257 imposed a baseline tariff on most imports, with higher country rates, under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA).

The framework holds tariffs to be good in theory but questionable in practice, used cautiously and targeted at leverage, strategic industries, and national-security threats ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Trade and Economics|Trade and Economics]]). Broad baseline tariffs go beyond that. On February 20, 2026, in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump*, the Supreme Court held 6–3 that IEEPA does not authorize the President to impose tariffs, and Executive Order 14389 ended the IEEPA duties the same day. That outcome fits the framework, which places taxing power with the legislature.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Supreme Court of the United States – [Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump (2026)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/24-1287%5F4gcj.pdf)

#### One Big Beautiful Bill Act
**July 4, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

Public Law 119-21 is a reconciliation law changing tax and spending; the Congressional Budget Office estimates it lowers revenues by \$4.5 trillion and direct spending by \$1.1 trillion over 2025–2034, raising deficits by \$3.4 trillion.

Lower taxes and lower spending match the framework's direction ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Taxation]]), but the framework targets a balanced budget and reducing the debt ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Fiscal Policy|Fiscal Policy]]), and the law adds to deficits.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Congressional Budget Office – [Estimated Budgetary Effects of Public Law 119-21 (2025)](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61570)

#### TrumpIRA.gov retirement accounts
**April 30, 2026** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14403 establishes TrumpIRA.gov to expand access to retirement-savings accounts for workers.

The framework moves retirement toward private accounts with default enrollment and opt-out ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Social Security and Medicare Transition|Social Security and Medicare Transition]]). The order adds access but leaves Social Security mandatory.

#### Control of Venezuelan oil exports and revenue
**January 9, 2026** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

After the capture, the United States took control of Venezuela's oil exports. Executive Order 14373 holds the sale proceeds in U.S. Treasury custody as Venezuelan sovereign property, with disbursements authorized by the Secretary of State, and shields them from attachment by creditors under a declared national emergency. The administration reported more than \$13 billion in sales by July 2026, with about \$3 billion disbursed by April for Venezuelan government salaries, oil-industry supplies, and other approved uses.

Securing the region and cutting off hostile actors matches the framework's direction ([[8. Foreign Policy & International Relations#Interventionism vs Non-Intervention|Interventionism vs Non-Intervention]]). The details need to change to fit it: control should defeat and contain the threat without governing the territory, and end on a fixed date unless Congress renews it ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]); creditors' claims should be settled through the courts rather than blocked by emergency ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]); the revenue belongs to Venezuelans, whose property rights hold whatever their government did, so it needs full public accounting, published audits, and spending only for their benefit; and costs to American taxpayers should be disclosed.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - The White House – [Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue for the Good of the American and Venezuelan People (2026)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/01/safeguarding-venezuelan-oil-revenue-for-the-good-of-the-american-and-venezuelan-people/)
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14373: Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue for the Good of the American and Venezuelan People](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/15/2026-00831/safeguarding-venezuelan-oil-revenue-for-the-good-of-the-american-and-venezuelan-people)
> - CNBC – [Trump Says U.S. Has Sold More Than \$13 Billion of Venezuela Oil Since Maduro's Capture (2026)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/trump-says-us-has-sold-more-than-13-billion-of-venezuela-oil-.html)

Executive Order 14162 withdraws the United States from the Paris Agreement and related climate commitments.

The framework holds that the United States is not beholden to accords that go against the freedom of its people ([[9. Energy & Environment#International Climate Agreements|International Climate Agreements]]). It still supports awareness efforts and names animal agriculture as the climate driver whose elimination also protects sentient rights.

#### National energy emergency
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14156 declares a national energy emergency to speed energy production and permitting.

Faster permitting and more supply match the framework ([[9. Energy & Environment#Energy Mix|Energy Mix]]). An open-ended emergency does not meet its rule that emergencies expire on a fixed date ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Emergency Powers|Emergency Powers]]).

## Government Structure and Executive Power

>[!tldr] In short
> 4 actions graded: 1 Aligned, 2 Directionally aligned (details differ), 1 Directionally opposed.

#### Department of Government Efficiency
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14158 establishes the Department of Government Efficiency to modernize federal technology and cut waste.

The framework matches government to its essential functions and abolishes agencies without one ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public-Sector Size and Civil Service|Public-Sector Size and Civil Service]]). It differs in detail: cuts follow lawful process, and abolished jobs come with severance or placement help.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14158: Establishing and Implementing the President's "Department of Government Efficiency"](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/29/2025-02005/establishing-and-implementing-the-presidents-department-of-government-efficiency)

#### Schedule Policy/Career
**January 20, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14171 reclassifies policy-influencing career positions so they can be removed more easily.

Accountability and a smaller workforce match the framework ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Public-Sector Size and Civil Service|Public-Sector Size and Civil Service]]); the framework limits at-will status to genuinely policy-setting positions and keeps a firm ban on firing anyone for party or political speech ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Civil Service Protections|Civil Service Protections]]). If the order reaches ordinary career staff rather than genuinely policy-setting roles, the grade drops.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14171: Restoring Accountability to Policy-Influencing Positions Within the Federal Workforce](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/31/2025-02095/restoring-accountability-to-policy-influencing-positions-within-the-federal-workforce)

#### Orders against law firms
**March 6, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally opposed**

Executive Order 14230 and later orders limit security clearances and federal access for specific law firms.

The framework requires individual charges and court process before the state penalizes anyone ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Due Process and Rule of Law|Due Process and Rule of Law]]). Directional rather than full opposition because the orders rest on security-clearance authority.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14230: Addressing Risks From Perkins Coie LLP](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/03/11/2025-03989/addressing-risks-from-perkins-coie-llp)

#### Citizenship verification in elections
**March 31, 2026** · **Grade: Aligned**

Executive Order 14399 directs federal agencies to compile and send states lists of confirmed citizens to verify voter rolls.

Matches the framework's rule that only qualified citizens with ID vote ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Voting and Elections|Voting and Elections]]).

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14399: Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/03/2026-06601/ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections)

#### Removing barriers to AI
**January 23, 2025** · **Grade: Aligned**

Executive Order 14179 revokes prior AI rules and sets a policy of American AI leadership.

Matches the framework's limit on AI regulation to existential and rights-violation risks ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology#AI and Automation|AI and Automation]]).

#### National AI framework and state laws
**December 11, 2025** · **Grade: Directionally aligned (details differ)**

Executive Order 14365 sets a minimally burdensome national AI policy and creates a Justice Department task force to challenge state AI laws.

Light regulation matches the framework ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology#AI and Automation|AI and Automation]]). Using federal litigation against state laws is a detail the framework does not address.

>[!quote]- Sources
> - Federal Register – [Executive Order 14365: Ensuring a National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/16/2025-23092/ensuring-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial-intelligence)

#### Strategic Bitcoin Reserve
**March 6, 2025** · **Grade: Mixed**

Executive Order 14233 establishes a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and digital-asset stockpile from forfeited holdings.

The framework keeps cryptocurrency legal and lightly regulated ([[10. Bioethics, Progress, & Technology#Cryptocurrency|Cryptocurrency]]), but limits the state to core functions ([[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Economic System|Economic System]]); holding assets is outside them unless forfeited to pay restitution.
