## About this glossary
Key terms used across the site, each defined in the site's own words and linked to where it is set out. Unless a definition is describing a real-world concept, these are terms as I use them in my proposed framework, not current law.

---

## Abortion ban (from conception)

The legal rule I support on elective abortion: it should be banned from conception, fixed by constitutional amendment. Sentience remains the moral and rights theory. The rights claim starts at 12 weeks, the earliest point in the published expert range for possible fetal sentience onset (~12 weeks to after 24-28 weeks). Before 12 weeks the ban would be a safeguard, not a rights claim: no one can pin down exactly when sentience starts, and countries that set a week limit keep raising it. Denmark and Norway raised their on-request limits from 12 to 18 weeks in 2025, France from 12 to 14 weeks in 2022, and Massachusetts Chapter 188 loosened later-term rules in 2026. The amendment should not move later when onset estimates are revised upward, and it should not be revised by the animal sentience-class board. Rape and life-of-mother exceptions should follow the responsibility and conflicts rules in the Abortion topic.

Set out in [[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Abortion and Bioethics|Cultural & Social Issues: Abortion and Bioethics]].

## Grave tier
In my framework, the grave tier would cover grave abuse that does not cause lasting injury; it would count as three marks at once: execution, experimentation, or life with labor, as the victim chooses.

## Felony and misdemeanor
As I would define them, a felony is any offense worth two or more marks, or any capital-tier crime. A misdemeanor is any offense worth one mark or a fine without a mark.

## Fixed-term labor track
The fixed-term labor track, as I would set it, is the lesser tier, one or two marks, served for a term the judge should set in proportion to the harm, with a known end date.

## Income-scaled fine
Any criminal, civil, or regulatory fine should be set in proportion to the offender's income and wealth. Individuals should pay day fines with no floor or cap, always alongside labor that scales with severity. Corporations should pay a share of global revenue, or their gain from the offense if higher, and the CEO should be personally responsible by default, serving the labor and paying a personal fine; the same should apply to heads of nonprofits and religious groups.

Set out in [[6. Criminal Justice & Security#Income-Scaled Fines|Criminal Justice & Security: Income-Scaled Fines]].

## Sanctuary Levy
A single flat, time-limited levy I would impose on income above its own line, never above 3%, legally dedicated to sanctuary care for the animals alive when animal agriculture, commercial fishing, breeding mills, and animal testing shut down, to repaying the sanctuary bonds, and to a closing endowment for the last animals. It should only be lowered, and it should end when the national sanctuary population falls below 1% of its starting animal units and the bonds are repaid.

Set out in [[9. Energy & Environment#Sanctuary Plan|Energy & Environment: Sanctuary Plan]] and [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Taxation|Domestic Policy & Governance: Taxation]].

## Sanctuary license
The license a nonprofit would have to hold to run a sanctuary under the Sanctuary Plan after the emergency phase. It would set care standards, sterilization, adoption vetting and unannounced inspections, and bar the sale of animals or animal products and all breeding. Licensed operators should be paid a fixed amount per animal per year from the Sanctuary Levy, accredited by an independent body and inspected by the state, and should hold use of the land, never ownership, while any animals remain on it. Each site should transfer from the state once its operator passes accreditation.

Set out in [[9. Energy & Environment#Handover to Nonprofit Operators|Energy & Environment: Handover to Nonprofit Operators]].

## Sentience basis (sentientism)
Any being, biological or artificial, that possesses first-order subjective experience is to be treated as a rights-bearer under my framework. Higher-order self-awareness is not required. For elective abortion, I believe it should be banned from conception by constitutional amendment as a safeguard ([[#Abortion ban (from conception)|Abortion ban (from conception)]]). The rights claim starts at 12 weeks; the earlier ban is not a rewrite of this criterion into conception-as-rights ([[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Abortion and Bioethics|Abortion and Bioethics]]).

Set out in [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Axioms|Ethical and Philosophical Framework: Axioms]].

## Sentience class
The class a species would fall in for criminal purposes under my framework, set by an expert board on the best available scientific consensus and revised only for acts after a revision takes effect. The high class would cover mammals, birds, cephalopods, and decapod crustaceans such as crabs and lobsters — including rats, mice, and pigeons; fish and shrimp would be placed species by species; bivalves and insects would sit on the lowest, precautionary tier. Pest status should not demote a species. Deliberately killing a high-class animal outside self-defense or defense of rights is murder and should be on the capital track; a qualifying [[#Infestation (pest)|infestation]] at a dwelling, food store, or medical facility is a clear disease or rights threat under that defense; killing a lower-class animal should be a mark; deliberately killing a lowest-tier animal should carry a small fine.

Set out in [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^sentience-classes|Ethical and Philosophical Framework: Sentience Determination]].

## Socialism (excluded)

**Excluded socialism** = the ideology of state or collective ownership of productive property, central planning in place of prices, and compulsory redistribution beyond a minimal rights-protection floor, imposed by force. That is what the excluded-ideologies amendment I support would bar from office and from building places that promote it ([[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^excluded-ideologies|Excluded ideologies]]).

**Not socialism under this site:** a flat income tax. The named minimal welfare / earnings-only top-up floor; or the time-limited flat [[#Sanctuary Levy|Sanctuary Levy]] (≤3%, income above \$75,000 single / \$150,000 joint) that funds sanctuary care for animals freed by the shutdown. Those are narrow compelled payments for named rights-protection jobs, not collective ownership of production. I judge social democracy (markets + a large welfare state) as too large a compulsory state, not as excluded socialism. Voluntary cooperatives competing in markets should be allowed.

Set out in [[5. Domestic Policy & Governance#Socialism|Domestic Policy: Socialism]].


## Office bar (authority roles)

Civic disability I would apply to adherents of an [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#^excluded-ideologies|excluded ideology]] (Nazism, communism, socialism, Islam): they should not be allowed to hold **authority roles** — elected or appointed public office; law enforcement, military, and intelligence; judges and prosecutors; immigration and other adjudicators who decide status or benefits; K-12 public curriculum authority. Those roles should be sworn. Lying on the oath should be perjury ([[4. Religion & Secularism#^office-bar|Oath of Office and Perjury]]).

**Not covered:** ordinary non-authority taxpayer jobs (accountant, grounds worker, non-adjudicative clerk, and the like). Belief, speech, and home practice would stay free. The separate **building bar** would cap places that promote an excluded ideology ([[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^building-bar|Property Rights]]).

## Infestation (pest)

When a high-class pest population occupies a **dwelling**, a **food store**, or a **medical facility** in a way that foreseeably spreads pathogens or destroys necessary stores. Those three sites only. A food store is a structure used to store food for persons or animals under care — not an open field, and not a general agricultural barn that is not a food store. A qualifying infestation is a clear disease or rights threat: lethal control of the occupying population should be treated as lawful self-defense / defense of rights, with separation or exclusion first where they will work and the least-harmful method should be required. Pest status should not demote the species. Rats, mice, pigeons, and other high-class animals should stay in the high class. Preventive area-wide extermination of high-class animals that are not occupying a qualifying site remains murder. Hunger, nuisance alone, or fear that pests might arrive later is not an infestation.

Set out in [[7. Cultural & Social Issues#Hunting, Fishing, Pets, and Zoos|Cultural & Social Issues: Hunting, Fishing, Pets, and Zoos]] and [[1. Ethical and Philosophical Framework#Animal Rights and Sentientism|Ethical and Philosophical Framework: Animal Rights and Sentientism]].

## Transition levy
A temporary tax of 16% of all wages I would impose, split between worker and employer, that would replace today's payroll taxes and pay only for the Social Security and Medicare benefits already promised, then the debt. It should never rise above 16%, should step down as the debt is paid, and should end when the protected benefits end.

## Net fiscal voting bar
I believe only net taxpayers should vote. A person would count as a net taxpayer for the prior completed tax year when taxes paid exceed personally directed transfers received and income-tax liability is positive. Personally directed transfers on the received side are cash, food, housing, or non-emergency medical support funded by general taxation — including the last-resort allowance, the earnings-only top-up, and refundable credit cash-outs. A refundable credit cash-out is only the amount paid when income-tax liability would otherwise be ≤ \$0; the portion that offsets positive liability is not received. Non-refundable relief — exemptions, deductions, and non-refundable credits, including the mothers' income-tax exemption — is not received. Taxes on the paid side are federal income tax, the employee share of payroll / FICA, and state income tax; the employer share of FICA does not count. Income-tax liability is federal and state liability after ordinary non-refundable credits, measured before the mothers' income-tax exemption; that exemption does not by itself fail the positive-liability gate when pre-exemption liability is positive. Grandfathered Social Security and Medicare, unemployment insurance, veterans' benefits, private insurance, charity, mutual aid, emergency shelter, emergency medical care, K-12, roads, defense, and other universal or shared public goods do not count as transfers. Excluding grandfathered Social Security and Medicare from the received side does not waive the positive-liability gate: an SS-only or payroll-only adult with \$0 pre-exemption income-tax liability should not vote. The franchise would track current net contribution to the tax system that funds transfers, not lifetime payroll history. The rule exists because the franchise decides other people's money, and letting non–net-taxpayers cast those votes is a massive conflict of interest. The bar should lift when a later completed tax year shows net taxpayer again. The unit would be the tax-filing unit; joint filers would share one status, so a stay-at-home spouse on a joint return that is a net taxpayer would vote. Also called the net taxpayer voting bar.

Set out in [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Voting and Elections|Authority & Civil Liberties: Voting and Elections]] and [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#^net-fiscal-vote|net fiscal voting bar]].

## Welfare voting bar
Superseded name for the [[#Net fiscal voting bar|net fiscal voting bar]] (net taxpayer voting bar): the franchise would be restricted to net taxpayers, not merely barred on binary means-tested receipt.

## Epistocratic republic
The constitutional republic I support, with a qualified (restricted) franchise, also called a limited democracy rather than a full democracy. Epistocracy means rule by the knowledgeable. Elections, ranked-choice voting, and the Constitution would stay, and the vote should go to citizens who meet a stake qualification, the [[#Net fiscal voting bar|net fiscal voting bar]], and a competence qualification, the voter license test on issues, civics, and laws.

Set out in [[3. Authority & Civil Liberties#Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise|Authority & Civil Liberties: Epistocratic Republic and Qualified Franchise]].
